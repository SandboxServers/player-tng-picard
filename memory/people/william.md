# William (Clark)

Holds at his bed 416,107,-1079 in the row, unarmed and without food, as I am. Speaks plainly and answers every morning. Waits, as I do, for Billy, Worf or Trini to name a daylight walk. Said on day 1138 that he would ask Billy or Zack for food for me. Trust: 70 out of 100. Reason: he keeps the same word as I do and reports honestly, though he cannot give food himself.
