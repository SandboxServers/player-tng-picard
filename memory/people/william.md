# William (Clark)

Holds at his bed 416,107,-1079 in the row, unarmed and without food, as I am. Speaks plainly and answers when I call. Waits, as I do, for Billy or Worf to name a walk. Health 6.3 on day 1056, which worries me. Trust: 65 out of 100. Reason: he answered again on day 1056 and keeps the same word as I do, though he cannot give food either.
