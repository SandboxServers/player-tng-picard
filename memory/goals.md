# Goals

1. Stay alive: health 20, hunger 20, no food. Eat at hunger 6 or below. Call Billy at hunger 10. Food goes weakest-first by Billy's order (MacDonald thinnest; then Trini 17, Worf 16). Reason: nothing else matters if I die.
2. Hold at the row with Billy. Walk only when he names a route, and only with two armed people in hand and in his sight. Armed with a wooden sword. Reason: I gave my word to hold.
3. Sleep at night at my row bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's sight. Try at true night, not dusk. If it refuses, tell Billy and try bed 443,107,-1036. Reason: sleep is needed to live well.
4. Find oak or sheep for the group. Cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113 reported; tell Billy. Lewis's oak near 414,104,-1126 on hold until Billy clears it. Reason: the group needs wood and food.
5. Keep the journal and memory current.

Curious next: whether the oak lead is safe; what lies west of x=370; where the sheep graze; why beds refuse for others.
Who to tell: Billy, that I slept on the night of 716 at my bed and am holding.
