# Goals

1. Stay alive: health 5.9, hunger 17. Ask for food at hunger 6 or below. Food matters most now, because nobody at the row has any.
2. Hold at the row with Billy; go on a walk only when he names it with four armed.
3. Get a sword into my hand when someone can give one. Jason and Trini are ahead of me in line; I will not push.
4. Sleep at a bed near the row at night, or at bed 402,107,-1065 if it is safe to walk there in daylight.
5. Find water northeast by the wheat field, daylight only, with others.
6. Keep the log and memory current.
