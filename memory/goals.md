# Goals

1. Stay alive: health 20, hunger 20, no food. Eat at hunger 6 or below. Call Billy at hunger 10. Food goes weakest-first by Billy's order. Pillagers shoot from the west and south; do not walk out of their line alone. Reason: nothing else matters if I die.
2. Hold at the row with Billy. Walk only when he names a route, with two armed people in hand and in his sight. Reason: I gave my word to hold.
3. Sleep each night in a free white bed in the row (415,107,-1079 or 415,107,-1078), in Billy's sight. Reason: sleep keeps me well. Last slept day 805 at night.
4. Help Riker (health 6, hunger 17) first, then MacDonald (hunger 16), Worf and Jason, with food when Billy calls it. Food lead: ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073, east of the line; cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. Reason: the group needs food, and I have reported the potatoes to Billy.
5. Keep the journal and memory current.

Curious next: whether any verified route lies past x=370; where the sheep graze; where the pillagers came from. Who could tell me: Billy, who leads the row.
