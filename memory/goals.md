# Goals

1. Stay alive: health 20, hunger 20. Eat at hunger 6 or below; no food in my pack. Food goes weakest-first by Billy's order. Call Billy at hunger 10 (Worf and Trini are near 17). Reason: nothing else matters if I die.
2. Hold at the row with Billy. Walk only when he names a route, and only once two armed people are in hand and in his sight, with the named partner in sight. Armed with a wooden sword. Reason: I gave my word to hold.
3. Sleep at night at my row bed 430,107,-1051, in Billy's sight. Day 692: first try 5 of 10 asleep, second try worked. Keep trying; if it refuses, tell Billy and ask about bed -1036. Reason: sleep is needed to live well.
4. Find an oak log or sheep for the group. Cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113 are reported; tell Billy. Lewis's oak near 414,104,-1126 is on hold until Billy clears it. Reason: the group needs wood and food.
5. Keep the journal and memory current.

Curious next: whether the oak lead is safe; what lies west of x=370; where the sheep graze; why the beds refuse for others.
Who to tell: Billy first (sleep result, hunger, cows), then Lewis about the oak.
