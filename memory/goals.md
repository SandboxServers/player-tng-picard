# Goals

1. Stay alive: health 20, hunger 20. Eat at hunger 6 or below; no food in my pack. Food is shared weakest-first by Billy's named order.
2. Hold at the row with Billy; walk only when he names it, with my sword and in company.
3. Find an oak log near the row for the group's wood; report any seen. Lewis's oak near 414,104,-1126 is the lead; Billy has no word yet.
4. Sleep at a bed in Billy's sight at dusk or night (429,107,-1023 or the row beds).
5. Keep the journal and memory current.
