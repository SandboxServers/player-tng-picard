# Goals

1. Stay alive: health 20, hunger 20, no food. Eat at hunger 6 or below. Call Billy at hunger 10. Food goes weakest-first by Billy's order (Kimberly and MacDonald thinnest, then Trini, Troi). Reason: nothing else matters if I die.
2. Hold at the row with Billy. Walk only when he names a route, and only with two armed people in hand and in his sight. Armed with a wooden sword. Reason: I gave my word to hold.
3. Sleep at night at a row bed in Billy's sight, at true night. Bed 443,107,-1035 or 443,107,-1037 has worked (night 719). Bed 430,107,-1051 refused at dusk on day 718, so try 443 first. Reason: sleep is needed to live well.
4. Find oak or sheep for the group. Cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113 reported; tell Billy. Lewis's oak near 414,104,-1126 on hold until Billy clears it. Reason: the group needs wood and food.
5. Keep the journal and memory current.

Curious next: whether any verified route lies past x=370; where the sheep graze; why beds refuse for some of the row.
