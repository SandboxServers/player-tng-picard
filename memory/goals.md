# Goals

1. Stay alive: health 20, hunger 20, no food. Eat at hunger 6 or below. Call Billy at hunger 10. Food goes weakest-first by Billy's order. Pillagers shoot from the west and south; never leave the row to fight, even one that is close. If shot, get out of its line, then call Billy. Reason: I died on day 813 by walking out to fight, and I lost my sword and banner at x=391.
2. Hold at the row with Billy. Walk only when he names a route, with two armed people in hand and in his sight. I hold no weapon now; I will not walk out armed alone. Reason: I gave my word to hold, and I broke it on day 813.
3. Sleep each night in a free white bed in the row (415,107,-1079 or 415,107,-1078), in Billy's sight. Reason: sleep keeps me well. Last slept day 813 at dusk; one sleeper of ten.
4. Help Riker, MacDonald, Worf and Jason with food when Billy calls it. Food lead: ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073, east of the line; cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. Reason: the group needs food, and I have reported the potatoes to Billy.
5. Recover my dropped sword, dirt, ominous bottle and white banner at 391,96,-1104 only with Billy's word and armed escort. Reason: they are mine, but the pillagers hold that ground.
6. Keep the journal and memory current.

Curious next: whether any verified route lies past x=370; where the sheep graze; where the pillagers came from. Who could tell me: Billy, who leads the row.
