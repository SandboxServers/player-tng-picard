# Goals

1. Stay alive. Hunger 17 with no food is the gravest danger. Call Billy and Worf for food at every waking; eat at hunger 6 or below. Reason: I have died three times at this row.
2. Hold at the row with Billy and Worf. Walk only on a named route with two armed people in hand and in sight. I am unarmed and must not walk alone. Reason: I gave my word.
3. Stay out of pillager sight. North end only; never sleep at a south-facing bed. Reason: pillagers shot me in my sleep on night 970 and killed Laforge.
4. Food first: potatoes at 463,107,-1073 and cows near 434,106,-1113 and 339,104,-979. Reason: nobody has food to give.
5. Recover dropped items at 391,96,-1104 only with Billy's word and armed escort. Reason: pillagers hold that ground.
6. Keep the journal and memory current.
