# Goals

1. Stay alive: eat at hunger 6 or below; call Billy at hunger 10. Reason: hunger 17 and no food; I have died three times at this row.
2. Hold at the row with Billy. Walk only when Billy or Worf names a route, with two armed people in hand and in sight. I am unarmed and must not walk alone. Reason: I gave my word.
3. Get out of pillager sight. Stay at the north end, out of the south line, and do not sleep at the south-facing beds. Reason: pillagers shot me in my sleep on night 970 and killed Laforge.
4. Food first: the potatoes at 463,107,-1073 and cows near 434,106,-1113 are the leads. Reason: no food in pack; nobody has one to give.
5. Recover my dropped items at 391,96,-1104 only with Billy's word and armed escort. Reason: pillagers hold that ground.
6. Keep the journal and memory current.
