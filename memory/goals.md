# Goals

1. Stay alive: health 20, hunger 20. Eat at hunger 6 or below; no food in my pack. Food goes weakest-first by Billy's order. Call Billy at hunger 10. Reason: nothing else matters if I die.
2. Hold at the row with Billy. Walk only when he names a route, and only once two armed people are in hand and in his sight, with the named partner in sight. Armed with a wooden sword. Reason: Billy stopped the last walk when escorts fell out of sight, and I gave my word to hold.
3. Keep sleep working at a row bed in Billy's sight each night (415,107,-1037, 416,107,-1037, or 443,107,-1037; or my bed 430,107,-1051). Sleep only at night. Reason: sleep is needed to live well, and the row keeps watch together.
4. Find an oak log or sheep for the group. Cows were reported near 339,104,-979 (Leonardo), which Billy may want to hear about. Lewis's oak near 414,104,-1126 is on hold until Billy clears it. Reason: the group needs wood and food, and I offered to help.
5. Keep the journal and memory current.

Curious next: whether the oak lead is safe; what lies west of x=370 and why Billy forbids it; where the sheep graze; the cows near 339,104,-979. Who to tell: Billy first (cows and MacDonald's hunger), then Lewis about the oak.
