# Goals

1. Stay alive: health 20, hunger 20. Eat at hunger 6 or below; no food in my pack. Food goes weakest-first by Billy's order. Call Billy at hunger 10 (Worf is 17 now). Reason: nothing else matters if I die.
2. Hold at the row with Billy. Walk only when he names a route, and only once two armed people are in hand and in his sight, with the named partner in sight. Armed with a wooden sword. Reason: I gave my word to hold.
3. Keep sleep working each night, in Billy's sight, sleeping only at night. My row bed 430,107,-1051 slept fine on day 689 (events returned, not a clear skip). Chapman reports beds 443,107,-1065 and -1064 let him sleep. Wesley's bed -1050 was taken by Jason. Before the next night, ask Billy which bed is clear and prefer one that is open. Reason: sleep is needed to live well.
4. Find an oak log or sheep for the group. Cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113 are reported; tell Billy. Lewis's oak near 414,104,-1126 is on hold until Billy clears it. Reason: the group needs wood and food.
5. Keep the journal and memory current.

Curious next: whether the oak lead is safe; what lies west of x=370; where the sheep graze.
Who to tell: Billy first (Trini and Worf's hunger, the cows, and the bed news), then Lewis about the oak.
