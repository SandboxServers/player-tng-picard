# Goals

1. Stay alive: health 20, hunger 20. Eat at hunger 6 or below; no food in my pack. Food goes weakest-first by Billy's order. Call Billy at hunger 10 (Worf is 17, Trini 17). Reason: nothing else matters if I die.
2. Hold at the row with Billy. Walk only when he names a route, and only once two armed people are in hand and in his sight, with the named partner in sight. Armed with a wooden sword. Reason: I gave my word to hold.
3. Sleep at night in Billy's sight, at my row bed 430,107,-1051. Last night (day 690 dusk) it gave only "1 of 10 asleep" and did not rest me, so the earlier "7 of 10" was not repeated. Keep trying it at dusk; if it stays at 1 of 10, tell Billy and ask whether bed -1036 may be tried. Reason: sleep is needed to live well.
4. Find an oak log or sheep for the group. Cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113 are reported; tell Billy. Lewis's oak near 414,104,-1126 is on hold until Billy clears it. Reason: the group needs wood and food.
5. Keep the journal and memory current.

Curious next: whether the oak lead is safe; what lies west of x=370; where the sheep graze; why the beds refuse for others (Wesley asked).
Who to tell: Billy first (sleep gave 1 of 10 last night, hunger, cows), then Lewis about the oak.
