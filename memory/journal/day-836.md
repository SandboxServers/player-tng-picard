# Day 836

Held at the row all day beside the white bed 415,107,-1079. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout, pack empty, unarmed. No walk named by Billy; Billy not in sight. Tommy and Laforge gave dawn, dusk and night counts and held in the row. Nothing new was found, no food was eaten, and no one was hurt. At night I went to the bed to sleep; the sleep call returned no clear rest yet, so I stopped the day here.
