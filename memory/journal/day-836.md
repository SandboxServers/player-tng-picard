# Day 836

Held in the row all day with Tommy and Laforge. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Billy did not come into sight and named no walk, so nothing was done beyond the counts at dawn and night. At dusk I went to the white bed at 415,107,-1079 and slept there, in the row's sight. Nothing was lost, nothing was gained. The lesson is that holding still is the job here, and it is safe.

People: Tommy and Laforge, steady and keeping their counts. Trust in both is high (about 85) for keeping their word to hold.
Billy: not seen since day 826. Trust unchanged; I wait for his named word.
