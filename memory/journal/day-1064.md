# Day 1064

Still at the north bed 416,107,-1093, unarmed, hunger 17, no food, pack empty. Billy and Worf stayed silent through both days, and no named route came. William Clark is holding at his bed 416,107,-1079 with health 6.3. I called aloud at dawn, midday and night and set no walk, because I gave my word to walk only with armed escort in sight. I slept at the north bed, not enclosed, and no harm came. Hunger is the gravest danger and it is not yet solved.
