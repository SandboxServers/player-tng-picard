# Day 673

Held at the row bed near 430,107,-1051 all day, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy is still working out the west route and has named no walk, so everyone stayed at their beds in his sight. Many row members greeted each other; Tommy arrived with potatoes and bone and is owed a potato by MacDonald's count, and Zack offered to share. No sheep or oak work. I slept at night at the row bed in Billy's sight; sleep worked.
