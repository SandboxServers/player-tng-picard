# Day 634

Woke at the row, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy called a named west walk for Picard and Trini to look at the blocked way at 413,107,-1050, then called it off when a bow was drawn to the southwest. Everyone held at their beds. I walked to my bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight and held there through the day. Nothing new was found west, and no food was found; the row is short of food and Troi, Tommy and MacDonald are lower. I slept at my bed in Billy's sight at night with no errand running.
