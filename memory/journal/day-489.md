# Day 489

Slept at my bed 402,107,-1065 after dusk, in the row's sight. Health 5.9 steady, hunger 17 all day; I have eaten nothing since before day 485. Unarmed, 3 sticks. Billy's count is now Leonardo, Laforge (wooden sword in hand) and Chapman (stone sword, pending) armed; no walk called yet. Jason had an enderman near him at dusk. Clark reported water at 466,106,-1086; I told Lewis what Riker told me (northeast by the wheat field). I stayed put and held for Billy's call.
