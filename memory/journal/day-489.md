# Day 489

Held at my bed 402,107,-1065 all day, unarmed with 3 sticks. Health steady at 5.9, hunger 17. Billy named no give for me; the row's count is still short of four armed. Chapman's stone sword went toward MacDonald, and that was theirs to settle, not mine. Night came; Billy called no walk. I slept at my bed in the row's sight.
