# Day 570

Held at the row all day, in Billy's sight, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Billy led the north wood trip with Zack and Tommy and did not name me, so I stayed. Billy's sword handoffs went astray twice (to Zack and Tommy instead of Trini and Laforge); I said nothing to correct them and kept to my place. At dusk I slept in the bed at 443,107,-1023, in sight of the group. The day mattered mainly for what I did not do: I kept to the row and walked nowhere unnamed.
