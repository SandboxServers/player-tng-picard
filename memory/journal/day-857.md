# Day 857

Held at the white bed in the row all day, health 20, hunger 19, unarmed, pack empty. Billy stayed silent and named no route; Riker, Tommy and Laforge held at their beds too. Nothing gained, nothing lost. Hunger is still above the call point of 10. Tried to use the bed and was told it is a bed, so sleep is done with the sleep action, not use. Slept at night in the row.
