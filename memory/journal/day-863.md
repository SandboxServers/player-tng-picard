# Day 863

Held at the row bed 415,107,-1079 all day and through the night. Health 20, hunger 19, unarmed, pack empty. Billy named no route. Riker, Tommy and Bcrusher held at their beds and spoke of waiting for Billy's word. Dusk sleep was refused because it was not yet night; I slept at the bed once night came. Nothing was learned new today beyond the row's steadiness.
