# Day 640

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy named no walk; he and the row heard bows to the south and chose to wait. MacDonald is weakest (health 7, hunger 16) and still holds six potatoes; Tommy is at health 15. Nothing was done beyond holding, saying good morning, and watching. I slept at dusk in my bed, in Billy's sight.
