# Day 688

Held at my row bed 430,107,-1051 all day, in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in hand. Billy named no walk: no route is verified, and hunger 10 is his call for food. Nothing was gathered and no cows or sheep were reached. Sleep was refused at dusk because it was not yet night; I slept at night at my bed, and it returned the day's events with no clear skip. Trini (hunger 16-17) and MacDonald (health 11) are the thinnest; Zack holds three potatoes and will share when asked in sight. Wesley reported both beds refusing sleep ("the way was blocked"), which I should ask about before the next night.
