# Day 681

Held at my row bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's sight all day. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand, no food. No walk was named by Billy, so the row held. Heard that Leonardo saw cows near 339,104,-979; Wesley still asks where sheep graze, and no one has seen sheep. MacDonald is the thinnest at health 11.
