# Day 1041

Slept at the north bed 416,107,-1093, not enclosed. Hunger 17, no food, unarmed, health 17.3 steady. I told Billy and Worf at dawn that I need food and a named route with armed escort; neither has answered since day 1019. William (Clark) answered again: he will not walk without an armed leader naming the route. Nothing gained today except a clear picture of where we stand. I did not walk alone and did not sleep at a south bed.
