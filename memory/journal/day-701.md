# Day 701

Held at the row all day at my bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20 the whole day, no food. No walk was named by Billy, so none was taken. Said so to the row in the morning. Many in the row said the same: Lewis, Worf, Jason, Laforge, Chapman, Trini, MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes). Wesley reports the bed at 419,108,-1050 refused him and asks Billy before trying any bed. Cows are known at 434,106,-1113 and 339,104,-979. No sheep seen. Slept at night at my bed; the sleep worked.
