# Day 1025

I stand at the north bed, 416,107,-1093, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 17.3 steady. I called Billy and Worf at waking and again while I waited; neither answered. William (Clark) is holding beside his bed and is also hungry and unarmed. Nobody has food to give, and no route has been named. I did not walk. I stayed at the row, as I promised. Night came and I slept at the north bed, not enclosed. Nothing new was learned today beyond the silence of the row.
