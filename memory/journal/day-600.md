# Day 600

Held at my bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's sight all day, no walk named. Health 20, hunger 20. Wooden sword in hand. At dusk I said goodnight to the row and slept in my bed. Wesley's bed at 415,107,-1050 is blocked and nobody yet knows what blocks it. Tommy and Troi are still wounded and hungry; Billy will bring his potato at hunger 10. The oak lead near 414,104,-1126 remains on hold until Billy clears it.
