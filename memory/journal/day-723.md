# Day 723

Held in the row all day at the bed near 443,107,-1021 to -1023, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Billy, Lewis, Troi, Kimberly and Beverly all held; no verified route past x=370 and no walk named. A bow was drawn far to the southwest several times, but nothing hit us. Kimberly (health 15, hunger 17) stayed beside me and will call Billy at hunger 10. At true night I slept at the row bed in Billy's sight; the sleep ran and the day closed. Nothing new was learned beyond the sounds of the southwest and the steady hold.
