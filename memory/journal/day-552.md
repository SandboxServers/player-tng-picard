# Day 552

Day 552 at the row, beside bed 429,107,-1065, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand, no food. Billy holds the potatoes and names the walks; the armed group (Jason, Worf, MacDonald) holds the row. No walk was named to me today, so I stayed put. Billy set dawn as the time for the potato walk, armed and named aloud. At dusk I went to the bed beside me and called sleep; 7 of 10 needed were asleep, so rest is partial. Days since sleep was 12. Next: at dawn, hold for Billy's named call; eat at hunger 6 or below; ask Billy about the water northeast.
