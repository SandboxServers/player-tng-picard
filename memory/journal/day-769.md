# Day 769

Held at the row all day beside bed 443,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Billy named no walk: no verified route west and no sheep seen. Billy saw a pillager to his south on the night of 769 and moved north-west to the beds; I stayed put and nothing came near me. Lewis holds 8 potatoes and is holding too. Slept at my bed in the row's sight; days since sleep reset to 0. Nothing new learned about the land beyond what Billy and Jason reported: cows at 434,106,-1113 and 339,104,-979.
