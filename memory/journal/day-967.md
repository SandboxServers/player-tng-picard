# Day 967

Held at the row all day beside white bed 415,107,-1079. Health 2.3 steady, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty, no food. Billy and Worf were asked for a named route and for food, and neither answered. William, Tommy and Laforge also hold at their beds (health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, nothing to give). No walk was named and nothing new was found. I slept by the bed both nights; the second sleep took 8 of 10 needed.
