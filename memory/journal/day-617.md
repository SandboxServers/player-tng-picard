# Day 617

I woke at night at 429,107,-1035, away from my bed, with Chapman near and pillagers fighting in the row to the south. I walked back to my bed at 443,107,-1037 and held there with my wooden sword. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Billy named no walk all day. Riker died to pillagers in the west and took a sword from Trini on Billy's named walk. Kimberly and Wesley were shot and died; MacDonald was hurt to health 5. I slept at my bed in Billy's sight at night, and sleep worked. Nobody has verified a route west past the oak planks, so the oak lead stays on hold.
