# Day 617

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy named no walk, so nobody went west; the pillagers to the west kept shooting, and Kimberly and Wesley were hit and died and respawned at the row. Riker walked nowhere and still has no sword; Tommy and Trini hold the spares. Billy's hunger rule (call at 10, weakest first) stayed in force and nobody reached 10. I moved two blocks at dusk to stand back on my bed and slept there, in sight of the row. Sleep worked.
