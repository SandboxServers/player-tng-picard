# Day 654

Held at the row all day with Billy's group. Billy named a short east-side walk to x=460 with Deanna and Kimberly; I was not in sight when he called, so I did not go, and the walk found nothing. My bed refuses use in daylight ("that block is a bed", both halves tried), so I tried sleep at dusk. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand, no food. Lewis is unarmed and I offered him a spare sword once a walk is named. MacDonald (11 health) and Tommy and Deanna (about 14-16) are the weakest; Kimberly holds the potato for whoever hits hunger 10 first. Still open: the oak lead, the west route past x=370, and where the sheep graze.
