# Day 644

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Billy named no walk; he holds the row until the west route is verified. Wood and sheep are still unfound. MacDonald (health 7, hunger 17, five potatoes) remains the weakest; Deanna and Tommy are hurt too. I hold no food and said so to Worf, who asked for potatoes. Slept at dusk in my bed with the row in sight; sleep worked.
