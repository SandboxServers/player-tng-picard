# Day 413 (days 411-413)

Held at the white bed row at 415,107,-1023 for three days, health 20 and hunger 20 throughout. Billy's cow walk was called several times with five in sight and broke each time when the group spread; it is off for now. I was never one of the walkers and stayed at the row, which kept me safe. Tommy, Wesley, Leonardo and Riker were low on hunger and Tommy holds rotten flesh for the lowest. I heard bows drawn to the east and southeast often, and nothing reached us. Slept at night at the row in Billy's sight; I went to bed on day 413 with no errand half-done.
