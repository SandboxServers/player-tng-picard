# Day 933

Night in the row. I held where I stood, walked one block to my bed at 415,107,-1065 and slept; 9 of 10 were asleep. Health 2.3 steady, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty, no food. Billy, Worf and Riker hold in the row and will name a daylight route at first light. Nothing new was learned today beyond the holding. Curious next: whether a verified route lies past x=370, and where the sheep graze. Tell Billy the bed count and my hunger when he calls.
