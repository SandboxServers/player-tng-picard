# Day 933

Held in the row all day at my bed (415,107,-1065), unarmed, health 2.3 steady, hunger 17, pack empty. Sleep at dusk only partly took (3 of 10 asleep); I slept again at the day's close. No daylight route was named by Worf or Billy, so no one walked out. Billy, Worf, Zack, Jason, Tommy and Laforge all hold in the row with no food, and Worf says I am the one hungriest first. Food lead remains the ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073. I said I would call Billy at hunger 10.
