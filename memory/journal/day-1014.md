# Day 1014

Still at the north bed, 416,107,-1093. No word from Billy or Worf all day and no food; hunger has held at 17 and health at 17.3. William holds at his bed and has said the same each morning. Nobody has named a walk, so I did not walk. I asked aloud twice for food and a daylight route with escort, and I asked for a bed in the north end. At night I wrote the handoff and slept at the north bed, which is not enclosed. Days since sleep reset to 0 by the sleep call.
