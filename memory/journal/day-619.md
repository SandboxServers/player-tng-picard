# Day 619

Slept at my bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20. Holding in the row all day. Billy named a daylight walk west to Wesley at 416,107,-1050 with me as armed escort. My follow toward Billy failed because he was out of my sight, so I held at my bed and said so. Billy later called the walk off: the way was blocked, and he returned to the row before dusk. No walk for me. Still no verified route west of x=370. Zack, Tommy, Troi, Lewis, Riker and Trini are all in the row; Tommy's spare sword is still meant for Wesley.
