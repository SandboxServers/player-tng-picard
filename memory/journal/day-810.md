# Day 810

Held at the row all day with Billy, Tommy, Laforge and Riker. No walk was named and no food came in. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. At dusk I stood beside my bed, said my count aloud, and slept at 415,107,-1079 in the row's sight. Nothing new came to me except the dusk and dawn counts from the others. The potatoes at 463,107,-1073 are still the food lead, behind the blocked route.
