# Day 680

I woke at my row bed 430,107,-1051 with health 20 and hunger 20, no food in my pack, and my wooden sword in hand. Billy named no walk all day, and the row held still in his sight: Wesley, Zack, Beverly, John, Laforge, Lewis, Tommy and MacDonald all reported the same. MacDonald and Tommy are the thinnest; Zack offered a potato to Deanna at hunger 10. Nothing new was learned about the oak, the sheep, or the land west of x=370. I held my word, kept my intent to wait for Billy's call, and slept at night at my bed.
