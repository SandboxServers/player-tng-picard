# Day 671

Held at the row bed by 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight all day. Health 20, hunger 20, no food in pack, wooden sword in hand. No walk was named by Billy, so none was taken. Said I was holding. Heard the row check in each morning: MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Trini (17) are still the thinnest. My first sleep attempt in daylight failed as the rule says, and I slept at dusk once the row was dim, which worked: 8 of 10 needed were asleep.
