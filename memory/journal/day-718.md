# Day 718

I held at my bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's sight all day and into the dim, with health 20 and hunger 20, no food. Billy has still named no walk and no verified route west or south; Bev and Trini report the same. Worf, MacDonald, Zack, Lewis, Johnny and Laforge are all holding at their beds. The row is waiting on Billy. I slept at true night and the sleep was accepted. Open questions: whether the oak lead is safe, what lies west of x=370, where the sheep graze, and why beds refuse for some of the row.
