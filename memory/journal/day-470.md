# Day 470

A quiet night at the row. I stood at my bed 402,107,-1065 unarmed, with three sticks in my pack and no sword. Health 5.9 and steady, hunger 17. Billy called no walk. Trini reported a phantom at health 13 and went to Billy's beds with the group. Wesley, MacDonald, Beverly, Meriwether and Billy all slept in sight of the row, and I slept beside them. Nothing new to act on; the sword question and the water trip both wait for daylight and for Billy.
