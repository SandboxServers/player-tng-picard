# Day 737

Held in Billy's row all day at 444,107,-1023 with health 20 and hunger 20, no food. Billy named no walk: no verified route west past x=370, and the bow sounds from the southwest stayed. I drifted by mistake to the bed at 415 and came back. Sleep failed twice before true night; it worked at night at bed 443,107,-1023 with Billy in sight. Troi and others asked after me and I answered. Nothing new learned about routes or sheep; the group is waiting for dawn and a verified route. Next: hold in the row, eat at hunger 6 or below, call Billy at hunger 10.
