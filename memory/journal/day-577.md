# Day 577

Gave my wooden sword to Tommy as Billy asked; I am now unarmed. Held at the row all day in Billy's sight with no walk named. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Billy handed potatoes to Tommy and set beds for Jason and others. Night came and I slept at the row beside the white bed at 443,107,-1051 without a bed named for me. Lewis's oak lead is still unanswered.
