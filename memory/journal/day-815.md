# Day 815

I stood in the row all day and all night at 416,107,-1079, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave. Tommy and Laforge held beside their beds, waiting for the same word. I said aloud where I stood, and nothing else came of it. I tried to sleep at the bed at dusk, but the sleep returned with no sleep recorded, so I stay awake for now. Nothing new was learned today except that the row holds steady and quiet.
