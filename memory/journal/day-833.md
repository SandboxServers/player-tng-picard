# Day 833

Night at the row beside the white bed 415,107,-1079. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Billy has not come into sight and no walk has been named. Tommy and Laforge hold at the row and report each dusk and dawn. I slept in the bed. Nothing new was found today, and I did not leave the row. Next: hold, call Billy at hunger 10, eat at 6 or below, never leave the pillager line alone.
