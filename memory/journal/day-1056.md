# Day 1056

Still at the north bed 416,107,-1093, not enclosed. Hunger 17 and health 17.3 all day, unarmed, no food, pack empty. I called aloud at dawn, midday and dusk for food or an armed escort to the potatoes at 463,107,-1073. Nobody answered and nobody came near. William (Clark) is at 416,107,-1079, also unarmed, health 6.3, and he holds to the same rule: no walk without a named daylight route. I told him neither of us walks alone. I slept at the north bed at night; it rested me but did not lower hunger. Billy and Worf have been silent since day 1019.
