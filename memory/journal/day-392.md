# Day 392

Woke at the bed row near 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Counted at Billy's centre with Kimberly beside me; she and Riker are low on health and food, and Jason and Data hold the flesh and chicken for the lowest. Billy called no cow walk all day, so I did not join one. At dusk I tried to use the white bed at 429,107,-1037, which failed because the block is a bed and must be slept in, not used; I then slept in the row in Billy's sight. Nothing hurt me. Nothing new was learned beyond who holds food and that the walk is still waiting on Billy.
