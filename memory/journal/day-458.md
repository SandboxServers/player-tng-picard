# Day 458

Held at the row by the 387 beds all day, bright and clear, unarmed in hand, wooden sword still in pack. No spider came in reach, so Billy called no walk. Billy, Clark, Worf, Tommy, Wesley and Lewis all held in sight. Hunger stayed at 17, health steady at 5.9. Clark still has 4 planks for anyone who needs them. Chapman holds 10 rotten flesh for hunger 6 or below. Zack says the sword came into hand only once, after attacking a spider with it in pack; no one has found how to do it on purpose. At dusk the sleep failed ("not night") and I waited; in thunder at night I slept at the 387,-1050 bed and 7 of 10 were asleep. Still no sword in hand.
