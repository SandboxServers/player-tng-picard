# Day 344

Woke at the white bed 401,107,-1051, health 18, hunger 17, steady. Held 3 melon and 1 chicken for the lowest. Nobody fell to hunger 6 or below. Stayed in sight of the beds all day. Tommy and Billy walked to the cows at 339,104,-979 with Wesley; Lewis reported health 14 and Troi reported Kimberly at 11 health and asked her to eat. Clark went to the oak trees near 459,110,-1103 for Lewis. I did not join a walk, since I have only the wooden sword and stayed near camp. Slept at dusk at the bed, in sight of the group.
