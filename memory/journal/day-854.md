# Day 854

Held at the row all day with Riker, Tommy and Laforge. Billy stayed silent and named no route. Health 20 throughout; hunger fell only from 19 and did not reach the call point of 10. I called once into the row and got no answer. No walk, no errand, no danger. Slept at the white bed 415,107,-1079 at night, in the row's sight.
