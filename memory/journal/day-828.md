# Day 828

Held at the row near the white bed 415,107,-1079, alone in sight but with Tommy speaking from the row. Health 20, hunger 20 all day, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named by Billy, who has not come into sight. I said plainly who I am and where I stand; Tommy answered that he waits for Billy too. Nothing was lost or hurt. At night I slept at the bed, in the row. Still curious about the routes past x=370 and where the sheep graze; Billy is the one to ask.
