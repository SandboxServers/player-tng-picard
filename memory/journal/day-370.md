# Day 370

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1051 with health 20 and hunger 20, empty pack. Riker's call was meant for Clark, not me. Billy never called the cow walk, though Data, Lewis and Riker stood in sight and offered to be counted. Nothing new was found: no water, sheep or real oak. Lewis is at health 14, hunger 17, and Data holds 3 chicken for the lowest. Slept at the bed row with others in sight.
