# Day 1022 (night and dusk, north bed 416,107,-1093)

Called Billy and Worf several times from the north bed; no reply. William (Clark) answered from his bed and is holding too, unarmed and empty. Hunger 17, health steady at 17.3. No food, no walk, no errand. Went to sleep at dusk in the north bed, not enclosed. Woke on day 1023 with no change.

Lesson: the row waits on Billy's or Worf's word; without food I cannot wait forever. Next: if they stay silent, look for food at the nearest lead by daylight, only with armed company.
