# Day 484

Woke at my bed 402,107,-1065, health 5.9, hunger 17, nothing in hand. Held at the bed as Billy asked, with no walk. Billy still has no sword-in-hand method; Laforge tried a new one on the bed and I have not heard the result. Enderman and phantoms harried MacDonald near the row; he dropped to health 2 at dusk. I stayed put and kept quiet. Went to bed at dusk in the row's sight.
