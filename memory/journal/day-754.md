# Day 754

Held at the row all day beside bed 443,107,-1051, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Nobody among the row has seen a verified route west, so no one walked. I told Billy I hold at my bed. Trini, Kimberly, Beverly, Laforge, Chapman, Lewis and Billy all reported the same: hold, no walk. Lewis and Kimberly are lower on food; Billy named the potato for Kimberly at 10. At night I slept in the bed beside me; the sleep worked and I wake on day 755 at the row.
