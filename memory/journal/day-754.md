# Day 754

Woke at the row beside bed 443,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Said good morning to the row and held still. Billy, Trini, Laforge, Chapman and Lewis all hold; no verified route west has been named, so no walk. Nothing gathered; cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113 remain reported only. At dusk Lewis and the others went to bed in Billy's sight, and I slept beside my bed. Slept through the night without harm.
