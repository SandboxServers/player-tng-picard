Days 261 to 283 were spent hungry. Wheat was the only real food path and it stayed slow: I planted seeds on tilled dirt by the south beds and the northern plot, and no harvest of mine was confirmed. Wheat meant for Trini's bread reached Billy instead, so the bread pool stalled and the handoffs were hard to verify. Beds often refused me ("that block is a bed") and sleep did not reliably end a night. Food came only as rotten flesh from Jason (I ate it at night, day 282) and a chicken that went to LaForge through Lewis, not through me. Scouting west, east, south and north found no cows, pigs or sheep; the north sheep field was empty. Salmon showed in the water, but the fishing rod never worked on water. A phantom came at the north shore on night 271; I survived with a wooden spear made at a crafting table. At dawn of day 283 I was at hunger 9 and following Leonardo's pig lead with the group.
