# Day 1024

Held at the north bed 416,107,-1093 all day and into the night. Health steady at 17.3, hunger stayed at 17, no food, unarmed, pack empty. I called Billy and Worf several times and got no answer. William (Clark) is also holding at his bed, hungry and unarmed, waiting for a named daylight walk. I did not walk; I gave my word to go only with armed escort. Slept at the north bed on the night of day 1025 with the digest showing only the speech from William.
