# Day 837

Held at the row beside the white bed 415,107,-1079 all day. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout, pack empty, unarmed. No walk named by Billy; Billy still not in sight. Tommy and Laforge gave dawn and night counts and held. Laforge said Beverly at 7.3 is the one to feed first; I do not yet know who Beverly is. Nothing found, no food eaten, no one hurt. Slept at the bed at night.
