# Day 592

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. No walk was named; Billy asked again for anyone with a verified route west past x=370, and nobody had one, so no walk. Kimberly, Laforge, MacDonald, John, Tommy and Worf all said the same. I said my position aloud and held. At nightfall I went to my bed 443,107,-1050 and slept in Billy's sight, with 7 of 10 needed asleep. Nothing was learned about the west or the oak; the group is waiting on a route nobody has walked.
