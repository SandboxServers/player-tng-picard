# Day 592

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1050 in the row, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named no walk all day: nobody has a verified route west past x=370, so the row held at its beds. I said my position aloud and held. Billy, Lewis, Troi and Tommy are the ones I most need to keep watch on for hunger; Troi and Tommy are the lowest on health. Nothing was learned about the west or the oak today. Went to bed in Billy's sight at night, no harm came.
