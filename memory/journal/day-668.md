# Day 668

Woke at the row, health 20, hunger 20, sword in hand. Went to the middle chest at 447,107,-1037 when Billy called the armed to gather. Billy named one walk to the oak at 414,104,-1126 with Zack, Riker and me as escorts, then stopped it when escorts fell out of sight. He now wants two armed in hand before any walk. I held in his sight and did not walk. Night came; sleep was refused in daylight and worked at night at the bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight. Nothing new learned about sheep or the west route. Thinnest still MacDonald (11/16), Troi (14/16), Tommy (15/16).
