# Day 668

Held at bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. Billy named no walk: there is no verified route, and yesterday's walk to the oak at 414,104,-1126 failed partway when escorts fell out of sight. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Trini, Zack and MacDonald are thinnest; MacDonald is at 11 health. Nobody reached hunger 10 today. I slept at dusk at my bed in Billy's sight. Still curious about sheep, the oak lead, and what lies west of x=370.
