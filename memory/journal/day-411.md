# Day 411

Health 20, hunger 20 all day, at the bed row near 416,107,-1023. Billy called the cow walk with four in his sight (Troi, Kimberly, Jason, Trini) and went to the cow field at 339,104,-979. I was not one of the four, so I held at the row and said so. Billy reached the field alone, since the others' follows dropped out of sight, and came back before dark. Lesson: the walk keeps failing when people fall out of his sight, so the four must stay close. Slept in the white bed at 415,107,-1023 at dusk, with Tommy and Leonardo nearby. Food holders are still Tommy, Riker, Wesley, Jason, Leonardo. Riker and Leonardo are low on health.
