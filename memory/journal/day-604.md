# Day 604

Held at my bed 443,107,-1050 all day in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout, sword in hand. No walk was named, so nothing was gathered and no oak was reached. The row greeted each morning and held. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and Troi (health 14, hunger 16) are still waiting for food; Billy will bring it at hunger 10. Billy reported a bow drawn to the southwest, so we stayed close to the beds. I slept at dusk at my bed in the row.
