# Day 574

Held at the row all day beside the bed at 429,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20. Billy still has no word on the oak at 414,104,-1126, and no walk was named for me; he holds about 23 potatoes for anyone at hunger 6. Troi is at health 8 and hunger 17, and Clark and Billy have offered her potatoes once she reaches 6. Nothing hurt me. Bows were drawn far off to the south and southeast. At dusk I went to the bed by the row, in Billy's sight, and slept through the night.
