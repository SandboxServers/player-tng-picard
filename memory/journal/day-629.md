# Day 629

Held at Billy's row all day at beds 443-444,107,-1037. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. No walk was named: Billy called off the west walk because bows were heard to the southwest. Chapman and Troi's potato for MacDonald (health 7) passed through the row by hand. Nothing gained, nothing lost. Oak lead and the way west past x=370 remain unverified. Going to bed in Billy's sight.
