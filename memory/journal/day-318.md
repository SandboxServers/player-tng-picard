# Day 318

Health fell to 2 with hunger at 17 and no food in reach. The camp fed me in turn: Beverly gave a raw chicken (kept for when hunger reaches 6), and Worf and Johnny brought melon slices. Reflex eating raised my health from 2 to 7. I thanked Worf and Johnny. At dusk I went to sleep at the white beds in view of others, but the sleep only reached 5 of 10 needed, and a skeleton was shooting in the dark near the beds. Lesson: food first, then the beds, and never alone in the dark. Still holding the chicken for when hunger falls to 6.
