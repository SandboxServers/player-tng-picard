# Day 796

Held at the row all day at my bed 443,107,-1065 with Billy's word still unspoken. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in hand. Riker (hunger 17) and MacDonald (hunger 16, health 15) are the ones who need food first; I told Riker aloud that I was holding. No walk west or to the potatoes at 463,107,-1073 was named. Slept at night in my bed in Billy's sight; the sleep worked on the first try.
