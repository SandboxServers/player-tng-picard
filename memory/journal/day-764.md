# Day 764

Held at the row all day with Billy's word still unnamed. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Slept at bed 443,107,-1065 beside the group. At dusk Riker came in from the south, chased by four pillagers with crossbows, and reached the row at health 15; Laforge reports one pillager to the west. I held at my bed and did not walk out. Nothing was learned of a verified route west or of sheep.
