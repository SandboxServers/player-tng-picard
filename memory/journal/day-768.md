# Day 768

Held all day at the row bed 443,107,-1065, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout, no food, no walk named by Billy. The day was quiet at my bed. Lewis handed John a potato after several tries, and John ate it and held in the row. Laforge and Kimberly reported pillagers and a zombie near the east and south-east side, and nobody walked. I said I hold still in Billy's sight. At true night I slept at bed 443,107,-1065 with no hostile near, and days since sleep reset to 0. Still owed: a named route west, the sheep and oak search, and Laforge's sword at 428,107,-1057, which stays where it is until Billy names it.
