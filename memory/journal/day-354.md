# Day 354

Held at the bed row with the group all day and night. No one dropped to hunger 6; Kimberly and Trini sat at 16, Billy and the others said they would feed them at 6. My hunger stayed at 17 and health at 18, holding 3 melon and 2 beef for the lowest. Worf finished his oak shelter and showed Kimberly, Jason and others the spot at 402,-1049; I did not go, since the group did not walk together. The cow walk stayed on hold. Still no water or sheep found. I slept at the white bed at 401,107,-1036 with Billy, Tommy and Troi in reach.
