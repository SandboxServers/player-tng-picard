# Day 798

Held at the row all day with Billy's group, no walk named. I stayed at my white bed at 443,107,-1065 in sight of the others and said where I stood. Health and hunger stayed at 20, with no food in my pack. Riker is at health 6 and hunger 17, the most urgent case; I told him I have nothing to give, and that if Billy names food he is first from me. MacDonald (health 15, hunger 16) and Laforge are holding too. Lewis has one potato kept for Chapman and one rotten flesh set aside for Riker. Potatoes at 463,107,-1073 remain the food lead, east of the line. No verified route past x=370 and no sheep seen. I slept in my bed at dusk, in sight of Billy's group.
