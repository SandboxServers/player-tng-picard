# Day 369

Died to an enderman near Billy's bed at 431,107,-1039 and lost my food and tools there; my items still lie at that spot, not recovered. Woke with an empty pack at the bed row, health 20, hunger 20. Told Troi I have no beef; Wesley and Lewis hold it for the lowest. Did not join a cow walk, since Billy never called four in sight. Slept at the white bed 443,107,-1050 in sight of the group. Lesson: enderman near the beds at dusk is deadly; do not go back to the drop while it is there.
