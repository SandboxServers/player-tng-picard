# Day 441

Still alone at the white beds 416,107,-1023, not yet named a cell by Billy. Billy's group of about ten held at the row (388,-1035 area) through dawn and dusk, and the cow walk was put off to a later daylight call. I held here, did not walk in the dark or alone, and slept in my own bed at dusk. Hunger 17, health 16, holding nothing. No food was passed to me, and none is needed yet. Lesson: waiting near the group in daylight works, but a cell has to be named before I can join the walk.
