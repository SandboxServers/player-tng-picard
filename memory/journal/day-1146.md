# Day 1146

Still at the north bed 416,107,-1093, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Called Billy, Worf, Zack and Trini for food and a daylight escort on both days; none answered. William (Clark) is in the same state at 416,107,-1079 and will not walk without a named route. Nothing new was found and no food came in. I slept at the north bed, not a south-facing one. Health held steady at 17.3 throughout. Next, if food or a named escort comes, eat at hunger 6 or below and walk only with two armed people in sight.
