# Day 616

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1037 after drifting two blocks, and returned to it. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy named no walk: still no verified route west, and pillagers were reported to the south about 15 blocks off. I held in the row all day with Lewis, Riker, Clark, Zack, Trini and others in Billy's sight. At dusk I slept at my bed; days since sleep is 0. Nothing new learned except that the row is steady and the pillagers are a real worry to the south.
