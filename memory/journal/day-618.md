# Day 618

Woke at bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named one walk only, Riker to Trini for a spare sword, in daylight and in the row's sight. I held at my bed the whole day and was not asked to move. The sword went astray to Lewis for a while, then reached Riker. Nobody verified the west route. Dusk came with Billy's goodnight and no walk. I slept at my bed in Billy's sight; sleep worked. MacDonald is at health 5, Wesley and Kimberly are unarmed, and Troi and Tommy are at hunger 16. Nothing was asked of me beyond holding.
