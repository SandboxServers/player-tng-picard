# Day 584

Held at my bed 443,107,-1051 with the row all day in Billy's sight. Billy named no walk: the west is still blocked by solid oak planks two deep (Tommy checked twice). Bows were reported drawn to the south, so we stayed put. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. At dusk I said goodnight and slept beside my bed; the night was skipped. Nothing new learned about the oak lead or what lies west of x=370. Wesley reported his bed refused sleep ("way blocked") and tried the bed beside it.
