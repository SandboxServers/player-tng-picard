# Day 517

Night at my bed 402,107,-1065, unarmed, with a stick in pack. My wooden sword went to Clark, then to Jason; Billy's morning count had only Chapman and Laforge armed, so no walk to the cow field. Leonardo sank to health 1.3 with no food, and nobody in the row had any; the chest at 405,107,-1037 was empty of bread. I reported to Billy and held at the row. Hunger is 17, health 5.9, still no food for me.
