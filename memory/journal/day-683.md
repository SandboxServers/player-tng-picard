# Day 683

Held at my bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's sight all day. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand, no food. No walk was named by Billy, so the row held. I told the row about the cows near 339,104,-979 and that MacDonald is at health 11, so he should eat first. An early sleep try in daylight was refused. At night I slept at my bed in the row. Nothing was gained, and nothing was lost. The row still lacks food and wood, and the oak lead and the sheep remain unknown.
