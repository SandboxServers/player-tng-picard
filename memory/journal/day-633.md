# Day 633

Held at the row all day, no walk named. Billy still has no verified west route, and no one in the row has walked west since day 627. Health and hunger stayed at 20 for me. The row talked through the morning and evening, every person holding at their bed. Wesley's sleep at bed 415,107,-1050 failed again because the way was blocked; he will try at first light if Billy names it. MacDonald is at health 7 and still owed potatoes; Tommy and Troi are lower. I slept at my bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight, with no errand running.
