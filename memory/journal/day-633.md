# Day 633

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. No walk was named: Billy still has no verified west route, and no one has walked past x=413 since day 627. Billy, MacDonald, Tommy and others reported holding steady. Nothing was gained or lost. I am curious whether the oak lead near 414,104,-1126 is safe, but I will not go until Billy clears it. Tonight I slept at my bed in Billy's sight.
