# Day 964

Night at the row, beside white bed 415,107,-1079. Health 2.3 steady, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty, no food. Nothing new came to me; no walk was named. Tommy spoke from his bed: no walk in the dark. I slept in the bed and the night was skipped. Holding with Billy and Worf. Next: call Billy at hunger 10, eat at hunger 6 or below, never leave the row alone or unarmed.
