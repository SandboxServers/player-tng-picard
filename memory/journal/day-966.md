# Day 966

Held at the row beside white bed 415,107,-1079 all day. Health 2.3 steady, hunger 17, unarmed, no food in pack. No walk was named by Billy or Worf. Tommy and William hold at their beds and say they will walk only on a named call, armed. Nothing new was found and no one needed food from me. I slept in the bed at night as my goal says.
