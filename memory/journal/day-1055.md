# Day 1055

Waited at the north bed 416,107,-1093 through two days. No food and no armed escort came. Only William answered, from his bed at 416,107,-1079, also unarmed and without food. Billy and Worf stayed silent. Hunger held at 17 and health at 17.3. I did not walk, because I am unarmed and gave my word not to walk alone. I slept in the north bed at night, not enclosed, and no harm came.
