# Day 1055

Hunger held at 17 all day with no food in reach; health steady at 17.3. I stayed at the north bed, 416,107,-1093, unarmed and with an empty pack, and called aloud for food and an armed escort three times. Nobody came. William (Clark) answered at night from his bed at 416,107,-1079: he is also without food or arms and will walk only on a daylight route named by an armed reader. I slept at the north bed, not enclosed, and did not walk anywhere.
