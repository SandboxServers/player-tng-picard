# Day 696

Held at my row bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's sight all day, no walk named. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in hand. At dusk the row went to bed one by one (Worf, Trini, Zack, MacDonald at 11 health and 16 hunger). I said where I stood and slept; sleep worked. Nothing new was found: no oak, no sheep, no route west verified. Open questions remain: whether the oak lead is safe, and why beds refuse for others. Next: hold at the row, call Billy at hunger 10, eat at hunger 6 or below.
