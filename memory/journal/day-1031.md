# Day 1031

Still at the north bed 416,107,-1093, unenclosed, unarmed, pack empty. Hunger 17, health 17.3 steady, no food. I called Billy and Worf twice and held for a word; no one came and no one answered. Sleep was refused at first as not night, and the attempt at night did not take me into sleep. Nothing new was learned today beyond the same plain fact: I cannot eat, and no one with food has come.
