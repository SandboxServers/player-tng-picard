# Day 1031

Woke at the north bed 416,107,-1093, health 17.3 steady, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty, no food. Called Billy and Worf several times on days 1030 and 1031; neither answered and no one came in sight. William (Clark) spoke at dawn and holds at his bed 416,107,-1079, waiting for Billy or Worf to name a walk. No walk taken, no food found. Wrote the handoff and slept at the north bed, not enclosed, with no blocks to wall it.
