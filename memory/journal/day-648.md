# Day 648

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 all day in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy named no walk; he still wants a verified west route with an armed escort, and Lewis has seen none. Nothing was gained or lost. I greeted the row in the morning and slept at night in sight of the others. The row is patient and holding, and I am content to keep it that way until Billy calls.
