# Day 648

Woke at the row at 443,107,-1021, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy, Trini, Lewis, William, Tommy and Deanna all held at their beds with no walk named, since no west route is verified and Billy will not send anyone west of x=370. Tommy and MacDonald are the weakest; MacDonald was told to eat a potato. I held in sight of Billy all day and waited. Bows were drawn again and again to the southwest, and at dusk I told Billy, who asked everyone to stay put. I slept at the row near Billy at night. Nothing was gained today except watching the bows; the sheep and the oak lead are still open.
