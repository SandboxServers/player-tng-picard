# Day 575

Day 574 morning at the row, beside the white bed at 429,107,-1037, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, sword in hand. Billy still has no word on the oak at 414,104,-1126, so no tree walk was named and no one went out. Nothing was asked of me and I held. Night came at 575; I went to the bed beside Billy's and slept, the row in sight. Nothing hurt me. The row is fed, the potatoes are held by Billy, and the one open question is the oak lead.
