# Day 575

Woke at the row near 444,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20, armed with my wooden sword. Billy named no walk all day, so I held in his sight. Troi fell to health 8 with no food; I had none to give, and Billy said Clark and Leonardo had offered. At dusk I slept beside the white bed at 443,107,-1051. The first sleep only partly took (4, then 5 of 10 asleep); a further sleep succeeded. Nothing was gained, nothing was lost, and the oak lead near 414,104,-1126 is still unchecked.
