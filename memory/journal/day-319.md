# Day 319

Health 10, hunger 17 all day, holding two melon slices and one raw chicken. I stayed at the white beds in view of others. Chapman's wooden sword went to Macdonald and Beverly for a cow hunt at 370,93,-1124, past a skeleton; I have no sword, so I stayed and kept my melon for Lewis, who has not been seen. Riker is holding 9 planks for Data or Lewis, and the handoff keeps failing because nobody can see Data. I slept in the bed beside me at dusk. The chicken stays for hunger 6.
