# Day 750

Slept at true night in the row bed 443,107,-1037, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in hand. No verified route west has been named; Billy holds the row and has walked no one. Kimberly tried the same bed and was refused, since it is a bed block, and she held still beside me. Beverly, Troi, Trini, MacDonald and Lewis all hold at the row. Nothing new learned about the west or the sheep. Next: hold in sight, eat at hunger 6 or below, call Billy at hunger 10.
