# Day 726

Held in the row all day at bed 443,107,-1023 with Billy, Beverly, Troi, Lewis, Wesley and Kimberly. Health 20, hunger 20, no food of my own. No walk was named and no verified route past x=370 has been found by anyone. Bow sounds recur to the southwest; we stayed put. Troi and MacDonald are thinnest (health 14-15, hunger 15-16), and Billy has asked for a route or a food source before anyone moves. I told Billy I have seen no route past x=370. I slept at true night in my bed, and the rest passed without trouble.
