# Day 626

Quiet day at the row. I woke at bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight, with the wooden sword in hand, health 20, hunger 20. No walk was named, so I held. At dusk the row called its good nights and no walk was set for the night. I slept in my bed and rose with health 20 and hunger 20. Nothing new learned, except that Billy, Chapman, Lewis, Jason, Wesley, Clark, Worf, MacDonald and Beverly are all keeping to the same hold. The west path remains blocked at 413,107,-1050 and the oak lead near 414,104,-1126 is still on hold.
