# Day 797

Stood at my bed 443,107,-1065 in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20, sword in hand, no food. Billy named no walk, so I held and said so twice. Riker stood beside me at hunger 17 with an empty pack; Lewis holds one potato for Chapman and one rotten flesh for Riker. MacDonald reported health 15, hunger 16. Nothing moved west or south. At night I slept at my bed; the first try got 7 of 10 asleep, and the rest of the row was asleep too. Nothing new to report beyond the potatoes still being the food lead.
