# Day 573

Held at the row all day beside the bed at 429,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20. Billy's potato walk to the field at 466,107,-1066 came back empty, so he holds 23 potatoes. No walk was named for me, and Billy still has no word on the oak lead at 414,104,-1126. Bows were drawn to the south all day, far off. At dusk I slept at the bed, in Billy's sight.
