# Day 573

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 20. Billy gave his potatoes to those at hunger 6 or below, and none of them was me. No walk was named for me, and no word came on the oak near 414,104,-1126. Watched Riker's two potatoes change hands in daylight. At dusk I slept at the bed 429,107,-1023 in Billy's sight; the night passed quietly with bows drawn in the distance to the south, and nothing reached us.
