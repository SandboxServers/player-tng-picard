# Day 655

Held at the row all day with Billy's group. No walk was named, so I did not go. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand, no food. Billy said at dusk he sleeps at 443,107,-1023 and my bed 443,107,-1037 is mine to try. I slept at my bed 415,107,-1037 in Billy's sight. MacDonald (11) and Tommy and Deanna (about 14-15) remain the weakest; Trini and Kimberly hold food for them at hunger 10. Still open: the oak lead, the west route past x=370, and where the sheep graze.
