# Day 372

Woke at the bed row with health 20 and hunger 20, pack empty. Billy had not called the cow walk. I tried to go to him, but he was out of my sight, so I stayed in view at the bed row. Riker called a walk to the cows at 339,104,-979 without Billy; I declined and waited for Billy's own word. Nobody walked. At dusk I went to my bed at 443,107,-1051 and slept, with Lewis, Data and Billy nearby.
