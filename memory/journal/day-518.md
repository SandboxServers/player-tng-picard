# Day 518

Held at the row through the morning count; Billy had three armed, so no walk to the cow field. Health 5.9 and steady, hunger 17, pack one stick, still unarmed. Leonardo died and woke at spawn with nothing, then recovered his wooden sword and torch; the sword would not go to his hand. Phantoms came at night around the row. I went to my bed at 402,107,-1065 with Clark beside me and slept there in sight of the others. Nothing new was learned about water today.
