# Day 518

Woke at bed 402,107,-1065 with health 5.9 and hunger 17, unarmed, pack holding one stick. Reported to Billy at the row and held still as he asked. Billy offered his wooden sword to me twice, but it did not land in my hand: the give failed when I was out of his sight, and by the time he reached me Tommy, Wesley and others had been given the swords first. I released my claim, as Trini and Jason were ahead of me. Slept at a white bed beside the row at dusk, no food eaten. Still unarmed, still hunger 17. The group is holding together, with no walk taken. Food is the real shortage: nobody at the row has any.
