# Day 823

Held at the row all day with no walk named by Billy. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, no food. Riker reported health 6.3 and hunger 17, still waiting on Billy. Nothing new was learned. At night I went to the free white bed at 415,107,-1079 and sleep began; three of ten needed were asleep. I stayed put, unarmed, in the row's sight.
