# Day 1059

Woke at the north bed 416,107,-1093, hunger 17, unarmed, no food, nobody in sight. I called aloud for food and an armed escort and waited about six minutes. William (Clark) answered from his bed at 416,107,-1079, also unarmed and hungry. Billy and Worf did not answer. I chose not to walk to the potatoes or cows without an armed escort, since I gave my word. I slept at the north bed as night fell. Hunger has not fallen, and no food has come.
