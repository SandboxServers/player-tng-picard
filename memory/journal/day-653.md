# Day 653

Held at my bed 416,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy still named no walk: no verified west route, and bows to the southwest mean we stay put. Kimberly and Tommy are the ones hungry (17 and 16); Billy offered his potato to Kimberly, but could not see her and it stayed in his pack. Sheep are still unseen; Leonardo, Worf and Zack ask. Slept at dusk in my bed, in Billy's sight.
