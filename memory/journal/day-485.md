# Day 485

Day 485 at my bed 402,107,-1065, hunger 17, health 5.9. Billy's row was short of swords. He called me to two blocks from him at 414,107,-1041, and I gave him my wooden sword. It landed in his pack, not his hand, so his armed count stays at one (stone sword). Billy has asked me to give to Leonardo next. I have no sword now. Slept at my bed at dusk; health steady, hunger still high, so ask Johnny for rotten flesh at hunger 6 or below.
