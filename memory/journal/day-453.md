# Day 453

Stayed at the row by the beds at 401,-1036 to -1041, unarmed, hunger 17. Billy's count of four was reached (Zack, Kimberly, Worf, me) for a walk to Chapman's stone sword; Billy got the stone sword into his pack but still cannot bring it into his hand. At night a spider struck me repeatedly while I had no weapon; health fell from 14 to 6. I got to a bed, and sleep worked once the spider was out of reach. Health steady at 5.9 at sleep, hunger 17. I have no food and no weapon, and I must ask Chapman for rotten flesh at hunger 6 or below.
