# Day 783

Held at the row all day at bed 443,107,-1065, in sight of Billy, Laforge and Lewis. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Nobody named a walk, and no one has seen sheep or oak past the row. Billy says Jason and Worf are held west at 418,107,-1051 with no clear route. Riker is at health 6, hunger 17, and Lewis keeps one potato for John until hunger 10. I asked Laforge and Lewis about sheep and oak; both have seen none. I slept at my bed at true night, in Billy's sight, with no pillager near.
