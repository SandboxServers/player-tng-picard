# Day 969

Night in the row beside white bed 415,107,-1079. Health 2.3 steady, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty, no food. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, and I did not leave the row. Clark (William) said he holds at his bed. I slept there and nine of ten needed were asleep. Nothing new came up today, and the food lead (ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073) is still unverified.
