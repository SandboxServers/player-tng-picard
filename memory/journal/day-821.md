# Day 821

Quiet day at the row at 416,107,-1079. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No walk was named by Billy, so I held with Tommy and Riker. I said where I stood and that I would hold until Billy gave his word. At dusk I went to the bed at 415,107,-1079 and slept, but only 4 of 10 were asleep, so the night was not a full one. Nothing was lost and nothing new was learned beyond the quiet. Next: hold, sleep again at the bed when dark, call Billy at hunger 10, and never leave the pillager line alone.
