# Day 576

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, with no walk named. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Billy fed Tommy (health 3) a potato; Zack stayed alone at the potato field and was told to come back. Riker took the white bed at 443,107,-1036 as Billy named. I slept at the bed beside me at 443,107,-1051 (8 of 10 asleep). Still no word on the oak lead near 414,104,-1126.
