# Day 576

Held at the row all day beside the white bed, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy led a walk to the potato field to feed Tommy (health 8) and to fetch Zack. I walked with them in sight and kept my sword, telling Billy I would stay armed. Billy asked me twice to hand my sword to Tommy; Tommy said he carried one, so I kept mine. Tried to use a row bed by hand and was refused ("that block is a bed"), so I slept at the row in Billy's sight. The oak lead near 414,104,-1126 is still unanswered. Days since sleep reset to 0.
