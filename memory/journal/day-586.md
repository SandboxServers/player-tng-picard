# Day 586

Held at my bed 443,107,-1050 all day in the row's sight, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 20. No walk was named: Leonardo has not reported the planks clear, so Billy holds the row. Billy brought two potatoes to Deanna, who is at health 13.9 and hunger 16 and is the one to watch. At dusk I lay down at my bed and slept there with the row around me. Nothing new was seen of the west; the oak lead near 414,104,-1126 stays on hold.
