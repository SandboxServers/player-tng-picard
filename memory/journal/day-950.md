# Day 950

Stayed at the row by the white bed at 415,107,-1079, unarmed, with no food. Health 2.3 steady, hunger 17. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I held. Tommy and William both told me that a white bed is used at night: stand beside it and sleep. I did that at dusk and the sleep took; days_since_sleep is now 0. Laforge, Tommy and William are all holding with no food to spare. Tomorrow: call Billy at hunger 10, eat at hunger 6 or below, and never leave the row alone or unarmed.
