Day 865. Held at the row all day and into the night with Riker, Laforge and Tommy. Billy never named a route, so nobody walked. I told Riker I have seen cows, not sheep, near 434,106,-1113 and 339,104,-979. Health stayed at 20, hunger drifted to 19. Slept at the white bed 415,107,-1079 after a second use of the bed made sleep take. Nothing new was learned except the cow locations.
