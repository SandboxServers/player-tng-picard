# Day 749

Held at the row all day beside bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in hand. Billy still names no verified route west and no walk; the row holds and asks for one. Troi (hunger 17) and Kimberly and MacDonald (hunger 16-17) are the ones who will need food first. No one reached hunger 10, so nothing was called. Bow sounds from the southwest continue with no damage. Slept at true night beside the row bed, in sight of Billy. Nothing new learned of the west or of the sheep.
