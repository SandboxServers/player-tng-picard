# Day 822

Held at the row at 416,107,-1079 all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave. Dusk came with no answer from Billy; at night I slept beside the white beds, and the sleep worked. Laforge said he was walking to bed 429,107,-1065 with no food and no sword. Nothing new was learned. Food lead is still the ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073, behind the blocked route.
