# Day 393

Held at Billy's bed row all day, health and hunger at 20, pack empty. Riker told me the water lies northeast by the wheat field; no oak trees seen east. Billy had five in his sight but did not call the cow walk, so I did not go. Slept at dusk; the night was skipped. My bed click at 429,107,-1037 failed, so I slept where I stood, and the sleep worked.
