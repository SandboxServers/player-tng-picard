# Day 666

Held at my bed 416,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. No walk was named; Billy asked everyone to hold in sight of the beds. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16), Data (13, 17) and Troi (14, 16) remain the thinnest; Billy has said he will feed them first if they reach 10. No sheep seen; Data reports cows only at 339,104,-979. Slept at dusk in my bed in sight of the row.
