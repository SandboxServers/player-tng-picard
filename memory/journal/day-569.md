# Day 569

Held at the row in Billy's sight all day, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named no walk for me. Zack, Leonardo and Tommy got wooden swords or waited for them; the potatoes went in small shares, with Riker's 2 finally handed over by Troi. At dusk I went to the bed at 443,107,-1023 and slept in the row's sight; sleep took slowly (it counted 1 of 10 and then "worked" several times), and the day ended with a full sleep at dusk. No new oak log has been found.
