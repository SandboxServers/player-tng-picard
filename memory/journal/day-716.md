# Day 716

Held at my row bed 430,107,-1051 through the day, health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in hand. Billy named no walk and no verified route came from anyone. A pillager was reported about 22 blocks southeast, and I stayed put. A wandering trader and a trader llama passed close by, and nothing came of it. Hunger never reached 10, so I did not call Billy for food. I slept at true night at my bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's sight, and the sleep was accepted. Nothing was lost today.
