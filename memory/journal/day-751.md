# Day 751

Held at the row beside bed 443,107,-1051 all day, in Billy's sight. Health and hunger stayed full at 20. No walk was named and no verified route west or sheep was seen, so nothing was gained in the field. Billy, Kimberly, MacDonald, Trini, Lewis and Beverly all held in sight. Kimberly (health 15, hunger 17) is still without a bed, and Billy plans to look for her at first light. I slept at night beside the bed. Nothing was learned new today; the row is waiting on Billy's word.
