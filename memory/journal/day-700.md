# Day 700

Held at my row bed 430,107,-1051 all day with Billy's row, no walk named. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in hand. A dusk sleep was refused because it was not yet night; I waited through the day and slept at the next night, and it worked (the night was skipped). Heard the row check in: Trini, Chapman, MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes), Lewis, Wesley (asks Billy about the bed that refused him), Jason, Zack (offers food), Worf and Laforge. Still no sheep, no verified west route, Lewis's oak still on hold. Did not call Billy; hunger never reached 10.
