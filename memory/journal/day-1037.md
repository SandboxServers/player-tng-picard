# Day 1037

Still at the north bed 416,107,-1093, unenclosed. Hunger 17 all day, no food, unarmed. I called Billy, Worf and William aloud; only William answered, with no food and no walk named. Billy and Worf have been silent since day 1019. I held still and did not walk alone, as my word requires. At dusk I slept at the north bed; the sleep worked, but hunger did not change.
