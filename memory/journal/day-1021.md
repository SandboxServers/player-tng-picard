# Day 1021

Stayed at the north bed, 416,107,-1093, not enclosed. Health 17.3 steady, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty, no food. I called Billy and Worf twice and got no answer; nobody came near. Clark is still at his bed to the south. The bed-use attempt failed because the block is a bed, so I did not truly sleep; the sleep call was made in the same spot. No walk, no errand. Hunger is the thing that must be solved first, with food from Billy or Worf or the potato and cow leads.
