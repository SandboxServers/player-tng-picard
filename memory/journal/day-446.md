# Day 446

Night at the beds near 401,107,-1036 with Trini beside me, health 14, hunger 17, unarmed. Trini died at the row to phantoms and lost her sword and 2 rotten flesh at 388,-1043; Billy plans to fetch them at first light in daylight with four in sight. I held in place and did not walk in the dark. My white-bed use failed ("that block is a bed"), but the sleep action worked and 8 of 10 are asleep. Next: at first light, hold for Billy's count, eat at hunger 6 or below from Jason, Chapman or Tommy.
