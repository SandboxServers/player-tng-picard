# Day 446

Day 445 I held at the row by the white beds through the night, unarmed, hunger 17, health 13.9. In the morning Billy's group fed Trini, who was at health 5, with rotten flesh from Jason, Zack, Chapman and Billy. In the afternoon Billy called the cow walk with four in sight and I was counted in, but my follow ended because I could not see him. The walk later failed when Billy drifted off the row, and at dusk a phantom came through. I went to the bed at 387,-1036 and slept at 401,-1035 when it was the nearest free bed, with 7 of 10 asleep. I learned that a follow ends the moment the one I follow drops out of sight, and that Billy will not walk in the dark. Still no food for me and no cell named.
