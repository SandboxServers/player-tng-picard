# Day 805

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 416,107,-1079, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Billy named no walk, so I did not move beyond the beds. Tommy, Laforge and I each counted ourselves aloud and waited for Billy's word. Nothing attacked. I slept at night beside bed 415,107,-1079 and the night was skipped. The food lead is still the ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073, behind the blocked route, unverified.
