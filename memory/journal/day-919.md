# Day 919

Held at the row bed 416,107,-1065 all day with Worf, Zack, Riker, Tommy, Lewis, Jason and Laforge in sight. No food in the row. Worf's north gap at 417,107,-1054 is open air, but nothing beyond it has been seen and no walk was named. Health stayed at 2.3 and hunger at 17. At night I slept at the bed; no harm came to anyone that the digest reports.
