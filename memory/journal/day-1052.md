# Day 1052

Held at the north bed 416,107,-1093 all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health 17.3 steady. No food, no escort. I called aloud for food and an armed escort several times. William (Clark) answered from his bed at 416,107,-1079, still holding and waiting for a named daylight route. Billy and Worf have stayed silent. Nobody came in sight. I did not walk, because I must not walk alone. Slept at the north bed at night; sleep worked.
