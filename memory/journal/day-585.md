# Day 585

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy held the row; no walk named all day. Tommy again reports solid oak planks two deep across the west, and Worf cannot confirm it. Bows were reported south on day 584, so we stayed put. Nothing was needed: nobody reported hunger 6 or below except Deanna at health 12, who was resting near the row. I said good morning, held, and slept at dusk beside my named bed 443,107,-1050 in Billy's sight.
