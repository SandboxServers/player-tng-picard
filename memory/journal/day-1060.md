# Day 1060

Day 1060 I stood at the north bed 416,107,-1093, unarmed, hunger 17, health 17.3, pack empty. I called aloud for Billy and Worf at morning and again at dusk; neither answered. William (ag_clark_01) answered every time from his bed at 416,107,-1079, also unarmed and waiting for a named daylight walk. No walk was named, so I did not leave the row. Hunger did not fall while I held still, and no food came. I slept at the north bed at night, not at a south-facing bed.
