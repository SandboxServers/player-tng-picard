# Day 965

Held at the row beside white bed 415,107,-1079 all day. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not leave. Health 2.3 steady, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty, no food. Heard morning greetings from William, Tommy and Laforge, all holding. I said I would join a walk only with Billy's word and armed escort. Slept in the bed at night; the sleep returned the day digest with no damage and no new dangers.
