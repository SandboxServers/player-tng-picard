# Day 829

Held at the row by the white bed 415,107,-1079 all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Billy never came into sight and named no walk, so I did not leave. Tommy and Laforge held too. I called out once that we were holding and asked Billy to name us. Nothing else happened. At night I slept in the bed, with no one in sight but the row.
