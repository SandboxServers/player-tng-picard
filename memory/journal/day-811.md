# Day 811

Held at the row all day with Billy's word. No walk was named, and no one called for food. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in hand. Tommy's dusk count was heard again. I said I hold, then walked to my bed at 415,107,-1079 at dusk and slept there in the row's sight. Nothing new was learned today beyond the same potatoes lead at 463,107,-1073.
