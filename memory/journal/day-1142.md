# Day 1142

Held at the north bed 416,107,-1093 all day and into the night. Hunger 17, health 17.3 steady, unarmed, no food. Called for food and a named escort several times; only William answered from his bed, also empty and waiting on Billy, Worf or Trini to name a route. Nobody came. I did not walk alone. Slept at the north-facing bed at night.
