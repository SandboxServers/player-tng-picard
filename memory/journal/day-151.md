# Day 1151

Held at the north bed 416,107,-1093 all day. Hunger 17, health 17.3 steady, pack empty, unarmed. I called aloud for food and a named escorted daylight route several times; Billy, Worf, Zack and Trini did not answer. William (Clark) at his bed 416,107,-1079 is in the same position, unarmed and waiting. Nothing was walked, nothing was eaten. Slept at the north-facing bed at night.
