# Day 1068

Woke at the north bed 416,107,-1093, health 17.3, hunger 17, unarmed, no food. Called aloud for Billy and Worf at waking, at dusk and before sleep; neither answered. William (Clark) answered each time, holding at his bed 416,107,-1079 and waiting for a named daylight route with armed people. Nothing moved today. I did not walk, and I slept at the north bed, not enclosed.
