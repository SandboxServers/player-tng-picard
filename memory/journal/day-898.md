# Day 898

Held at the row bed 416,107,-1079 all day with Laforge beside me; Tommy and Riker held at their beds too. Health 20, hunger 19, pack empty, unarmed. Billy has named no walk and is out of sight. Sleep at dusk failed because it was not yet night; at night one sleep gave "4 of 10 asleep", and a second sleep worked. Nothing new was learned about the ground beyond the row. Laforge reports pillagers near the row, and I told him I would not walk out.
