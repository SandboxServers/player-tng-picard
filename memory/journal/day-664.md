# Day 664

Held at my bed 416,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. No walk was named, so nothing was gone after. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Heard the row check in each morning and night; MacDonald (health 11) and Data (13) are the thinnest. Leonardo and Data report no sheep seen; only cows at 339,104,-979. Billy handed the wooden sword to John and went to bed beside John's row. Slept at dusk in sight of the row.
