# Day 460

Held at the bed 387,107,-1051 in sight of Billy's row all day. Sleep on day 459 only partly took (3 of 10 asleep), so I woke in daylight with nothing new. Health 5.9 steady, hunger 17, no food in my pack. Billy has not called a walk. No spider came in reach, so the sword method was not tried. Chapman holds 10 rotten flesh for hunger 6 or below. Went to bed at dusk on day 460 at my bed, in sight of the row. Water northeast by the wheat field is still unwalked.
