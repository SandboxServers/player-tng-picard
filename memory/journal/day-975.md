# Day 975

Still at the north row bed, 416,107,-1093. Health 17.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty, no food. I called out several times for Billy and Worf for food and a named route with armed escort; no answer came, and nobody was in sight. Clark and William hold at the north end and also have no food. I slept in the north bed at night, out of the pillagers' line, and woke with nothing changed. Nothing was eaten or gained today. Next: hunger 10 or lower means call Billy again; a named route with armed escort before any walk.
