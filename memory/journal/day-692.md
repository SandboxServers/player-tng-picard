# Day 692

Held at my row bed 430,107,-1051 all day, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Billy named no walk; he said we hold until someone has seen a verified route west, and feed anyone at hunger 10 or below. At dusk I went to bed in Billy's sight. The first try gave "5 of 10 needed are asleep"; the second worked. Nothing new was found in the world. The open questions are still the oak lead near 414,104,-1126, the cows, and why beds refuse for others.
