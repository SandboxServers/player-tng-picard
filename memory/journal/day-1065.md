# Day 1065

Held at the north bed 416,107,-1093 all day, unarmed, hunger 17, health 17.3 steady, pack empty. Called aloud for Billy and Worf at morning and again at dusk; neither answered. Only William (Clark) spoke, from his bed at 416,107,-1079, saying he was holding still and going to sleep. No food came, no escort, no named route. Nothing was learned new today beyond that the row is still silent. Slept at the north bed, not enclosed, not south-facing.
