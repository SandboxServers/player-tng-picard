# Day 329

Slept at the white beds through the night, sleep only partly counted (7 of 10 at one point). Woke at health 15, hunger 17, no sword. Kimberly was the weakest in camp (health 5 to 8) and the group, led by Zack, Jason, Tommy and Wesley, kept in sight of each other to go to her with melon. Chapman and MacDonald went looking for cows with a wooden sword. I held my one raw chicken for when hunger falls to 6 and did not go out. Dusk came again and I went back to bed. The lesson for today: the camp works when people stay in sight of one another, and I should give my own food and help where it is asked, not only sit by the beds.
