Day 296 (latest in pending.md; days 283 to 296). Hunger was the thread through the whole stretch. From day 283 I lived on scraps: rotten flesh from Trini, one bread made from wheat pooled by Trini, Kimberly and Jason, a few porkchops and a beef from Riker's hunt on day 292. Wheat I planted took root but did not ripen for me. Beds kept refusing me ("that block is a bed"), so I slept at the white beds on some nights, walled in with planks on others. On night 292 I built a plank shelter at the east pig area, was caught by phantoms at health 5 and died with nothing; I respawned at the white beds with no items and had to rebuild from logs. Handoffs were the other lesson: wheat I gave to Clark on day 296 vanished from my pack before it reached him, and wheat meant for Trini reached Billy. Animals were not found in any direction I scouted. The group's shared food, not my own search, kept me alive. Phantoms at night caused most of my harm.
