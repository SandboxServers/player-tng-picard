# Day 827

Held at the row all day beside the white beds, around 416,107,-1079. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Billy never came into sight and no walk was named. Laforge held at bed 430,107,-1065 and asked Billy to feed Riker and MacDonald first. Nothing was learned and nothing was lost. I slept in the bed at dusk, as my goal says, with Billy out of sight.
