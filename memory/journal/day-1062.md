# Day 1062

Slept at the north bed on the night of 1061 and woke with hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty, no food. Health held steady at 17.3 all day, which is not regeneration. Called aloud for food and an armed escort at first light and again at dawn. William (Clark) answered that he is holding at his bed, unarmed and hungry. Billy and Worf have sent no word since day 1019 and were not in sight. I did not walk anywhere alone, as I promised. Going to rest now with no errand running, not enclosed, at the north bed.
