# Day 775

Woke at the bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in hand. Billy led the row and named no walk: no verified route west, no sheep news. Riker is mending after a potato from Lewis. John (Chapman) is at health 11 and hunger 17 and was given a potato by Lewis. Laforge and Lewis hold still at the beds. I had nothing to give. No pillager or zombie came near. I said good morning, told Billy I hold until he names a route, and at night I slept at my bed in the row, in Billy's sight. Still no food for me, and I am still curious where the sheep graze and where the pillagers came from.
