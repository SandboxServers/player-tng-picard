# Day 636

Woke at bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. The row held all day with no walk called and no west route verified. Zack reported no sheep seen. At night the row went to bed one by one in Billy's sight, and I slept at my bed too. Nothing new was learned about the oak lead, the blocked path at 413,107,-1050, or the sheep. Nothing hurt me.
