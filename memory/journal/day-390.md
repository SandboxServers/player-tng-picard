# Day 390

Held at the bed row all day in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. Billy has still not called the cow walk; I joined his count several times and held. Checked Worf's pit edge at 398,107,-1028 with him: no bare stone seen at 394,107,-1025, so his cell stays unconfirmed and I dug nothing. Phantom seen 14 blocks south at 425,120,-1030, so we stayed near the row. Days since sleep had reached 17; I finally slept in the white bed at 429,107,-1051 at night, 9 of 10 asleep with me. Nothing was lost today.
