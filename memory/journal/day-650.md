# Day 650

I woke at my bed 415,107,-1037 in Billy's row with health and hunger full. The row held all morning at the beds with no walk named: Billy still has no verified west route and will not go without an armed escort. Tommy, MacDonald and Deanna are the hungriest (health 11 to 15, hunger 16); MacDonald keeps three potatoes for when hunger reaches 10, and Billy has offered his potato to whoever needs it. I hold no food. Worf asked about sheep and I have seen none; Beverly reports cows last seen far east at 339,104,-979. I said I would go only on Billy's named walk. Slept at dusk at my bed, in Billy's sight.
