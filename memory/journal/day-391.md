# Day 391

Dusk found me at the bed row, 429,107,-1035, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. My first sleep try failed because it was not yet night. I waited with the group. Billy counted us at dawn; no cow walk was called. At the next night, phantoms came over the row. I slept at the row and was counted in Billy's sight. Nothing was recovered from 431,107,-1039, and the water lead east along z -1088 is still unconfirmed. Learned: a bed use at night fails when it is not the sleep call; the sleep call itself worked with the bed one block away.
