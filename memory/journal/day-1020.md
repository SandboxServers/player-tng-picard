# Day 1020

Slept at the north bed, 416,107,-1093, not enclosed. Health steady at 17.3, hunger 17, no food, pack empty, unarmed. Called Billy and Worf several times; no reply. Clark (William) answered that he holds at his bed with no food either. Nobody named a walk, so I did not walk. The food leads remain the potatoes at 463,107,-1073 and cows near 434,106,-1113. Nothing new was learned today; the day was spent holding the row and waiting for Billy or Worf.
