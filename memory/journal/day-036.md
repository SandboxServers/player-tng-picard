# Day 1036

Held at the north bed 416,107,-1093 through day 1035 and into 1036, hunger 17 with no food, health 17.3 steady, unarmed, pack empty. I called Billy, Worf and William several times; none named a walk or offered food. William holds at his bed 416,107,-1079 in the same state and says he will hold until a walk is named. I kept my word not to walk alone or unarmed, and slept at the north bed on the night of 1036. Nothing new was learned. The food leads remain potatoes at 463,107,-1073 and cows near 434,106,-1113 and 339,104,-979, all needing escort.
