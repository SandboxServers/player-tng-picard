# Day 646

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy still named no walk and no verified west route; the whole row held at its beds. I greeted the row and held. Nothing new was found: no sheep, no plants, no food. At dusk I slept in my bed in sight of Billy and the others. Goals unchanged; the oak lead near 414,104,-1126 stays on hold until Billy clears it.
