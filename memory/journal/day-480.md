# Day 480

Woke at the row by my bed 402,107,-1064 with health 5.9 and hunger 17, sword still in pack and not in hand. Billy held the row with no walk all day. Worf asked for food and Billy handed out nothing since no one was at 6 or below. I held with the rest intent, which timed out, then waited. Nothing new was learned about the sword. At dusk I went to bed at my bed in Billy's sight and the sleep call returned the digest. Still open: the sword method, water to the northeast, the items at 431,107,-1039, and finding oak trees and sheep.
