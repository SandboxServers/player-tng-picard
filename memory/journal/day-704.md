# Day 704

Held at my row bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's sight all day. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in hand. No walk was named and no errand ran. Trini said good night from her bed and noted Lewis close by. At true night I slept at my bed; the night was skipped and I woke sheltered and well. Open questions remain: the oak lead near 414,104,-1126 (on hold), where the sheep graze, and why beds refuse for others. Next: hold, call Billy at hunger 10, eat at 6 or below, sleep at my bed at night.
