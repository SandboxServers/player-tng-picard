# Day 852

Held at the white bed in the row all day, health 20, hunger 19, pack empty, unarmed. Billy never called a route, so no one walked out. Riker, Tommy and Laforge held beside me, each waiting for Billy's named word, and all stayed. Nothing new was learned from the outside. I slept in the bed at night, as the goal says, with the row in sight. Still open: the route past x=370, where the sheep graze, and where the pillagers came from. Billy is the one to ask.
