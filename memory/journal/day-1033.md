# Day 1033-1034

Held at the north bed 416,107,-1093 through the night and the next day. Hunger stayed at 17 with no food, pack empty, unarmed. I called Billy and Worf several times; neither answered, and neither was in sight. William (Clark) answered and holds at his bed 416,107,-1079, also with no food. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Slept at the north bed at dusk on day 1034 with a handoff written. Nothing new was found.
