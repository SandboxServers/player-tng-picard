Day 714. I held at my row bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's sight the whole day, health 20 and hunger 20 with no food. Billy named no walk and no one has a verified route west or south. I did not sleep the night of 712 or 713; the dusks passed while I held, so days_since_sleep is high. I slept on the night of 714 at my bed and the sleep worked. MacDonald (health 11) ate a potato on Billy's word; Zack offers three potatoes and says the thinnest eat first. Trini (17) and Worf (16) are the next to call. Nothing was gained or lost by me in the row today.
