# Day 780

Held at the row all day at the beds, 443,107,-1065, in Billy's sight. Health and hunger stayed at 20, with no food. Billy called no walk: no checked route west and no sheep sighted. Riker, Laforge and Lewis all held too. Cow marks at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113 were noted again. I asked Billy about a route and sheep, and he answered. At night I slept at the bed, 0 days since sleep.
