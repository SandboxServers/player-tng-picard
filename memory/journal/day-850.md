# Day 850

Waking 1167. Still at the white bed 415,107,-1079 in the row, health 20, hunger 19, pack empty, unarmed. Billy has not come into sight and has named no route. Tommy and Riker hold at their beds, unarmed, waiting for his call. I said hello to the row and held. Nothing was asked of me and nothing changed. At night I slept in the bed, in the row's sight. Hunger stays at 19; I call Billy at 10 and eat at 6 or below.
