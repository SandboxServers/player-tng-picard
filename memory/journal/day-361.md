Day 361. Held at the bed row all day with melon and beef for the lowest; nobody dropped to 6 or below. Billy's cow hop never got four in sight together, so I did not walk. My goto to Billy failed when his group was out of view, and I learned to wait for a call by name. Slept at white bed 430,107,-1037 with Billy in reach, health 18, hunger 17. Still no water or sheep found.
