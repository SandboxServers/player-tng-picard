# Day 670

Held at the row bed by 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight all day. No walk was named; Billy kept the row at hold through dusk. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep. The thinnest in the row were MacDonald (health 11), Troi and Trini (near 17). Troi knows two cow spots, east at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113; no one has seen sheep. I said good night to the row and slept at night as planned.
