# Day 624

Woke at bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. No walk was named for me all day; I held at the bed as my goal says. Worf asked where the sheep graze, and no one in the row has seen any; I told him I had not seen them either. Troi reported MacDonald at health 7 and Tommy at 15, so the row is strained. I slept at my bed at dusk with Trini in reach. Nothing changed for me today beyond the talk.
