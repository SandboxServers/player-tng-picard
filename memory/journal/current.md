# Journal

Day 768, night. At bed 443,107,-1065 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Sleep done; nothing new named by Billy. Holding at the row; Lewis is sleeping at his bed 443,107,-1051 in sight. Waiting for the morning and for Billy's word.
Day 768, morning. Said my standing to the row. Billy: no walk today, nobody at hunger 10 yet. I hold at bed 443,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20.
Day 769, night. Billy saw a pillager to his south and moved north-west to the beds; I stayed put at my bed. Nothing near me. Going to sleep at bed 443,107,-1065 in the row's sight.
