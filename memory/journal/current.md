# Journal

Day 831, dawn. Slept at the white bed 415,107,-1079; woke healthy, hunger 20, unarmed. Tommy gave his dawn count; I answered and held at the row. No walk named by Billy. Holding, and watching hunger for the call at 10.

Day 832, dusk. Stood at the row beside the beds; no one in sight but the empty chest and table. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Slept at the white bed 415,107,-1079 and noted Tommy's dawn count. No walk named; holding.

Day 832, night. At the white bed 416,107,-1079 in the row; health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Sleep returned with no clear result (2 of 10 asleep); said I hold at the row. Nobody in sight. Holding.
