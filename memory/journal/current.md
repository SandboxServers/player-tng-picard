# Journal
- Day 963, morning. Slept at the row beside white bed 415,107,-1079; night skipped. Health 2.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Clark and Tommy awake at the row; no walk named. Said good morning and that I hold for Billy or Worf's named walk. Waited through the day; nothing new. Night now; going to my bed to sleep. Hunger still 17, so no call to Billy yet.
