# Journal
- Day 585, night. Stood beside my bed 443,107,-1050 in Billy's sight, then slept there. Health 20, hunger 20.
- Morning. Billy asked the row what they have seen of the west; Tommy's last sight was oak planks. I told Billy I have not seen the west myself. The row answered the same: nobody has seen it clear. Deanna is at health 12, hunger 17, and needs watching. Holding at my bed until Billy names a route.
