# Journal
- Day 827, dusk. At white bed 415,107,-1079. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Sleep refused: not yet night. No one in sight; holding at the row, waiting for Billy.
- Night came. Slept at the bed 415,107,-1079. Tommy holds at the row and will not walk in the dark. Billy still not in sight; no walk named. Nothing lost, nothing hurt.
