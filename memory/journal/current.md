Day 917, night. At the row bed 415,107,-1065 (beside it at 416,107,-1065), not enclosed, health 2.3 steady, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Billy reports a phantom to the north-west hit him twice; Billy, Worf and Zack hold near 417,107,-1050. No walk at night.
