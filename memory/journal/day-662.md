# Day 662

Held at my bed 416,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout, no food in my pack. MacDonald (health 11) and Data (13) are thin; Troi offered to keep a potato for MacDonald if he reaches hunger 10 first. Sheep still not found; Data has seen only cows far east near 339,104,-979. I slept at dusk in Billy's sight and the sleep worked.
