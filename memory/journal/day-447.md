# Day 447

Held at the beds near the row all day, unarmed and holding nothing, hunger 17, health steady near 14. Billy did not call the cow walk; his group was sorting out Trini's lost sword and the jungle planks meant for Leonardo, and the planks went astray more than once. I stayed out of that errand and said so plainly. At dusk I went to the bed at 401,-1036 in the row's sight, but the bed use failed and I slept from beside it. Nothing came of the day's walks, and I learned that the camp's food and planks change hands without much record.
