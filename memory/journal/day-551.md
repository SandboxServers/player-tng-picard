# Day 551

Held at the row by the beds at 430,107,-1065 through dusk. Billy's potato walk went out with Jason, Worf and MacDonald, and I was not named for it. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in hand. Slept at the bed in the row's sight after dark; the sleep worked. Still no sword of my own and no food of my own. Next: dawn, and only on a walk Billy names.
