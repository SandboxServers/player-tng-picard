# Day 478

Slept at my bed 402,107,-1065 and woke with the row at first light. Health 5.9, hunger 17, no damage taken. Sword still in pack and not in hand; no one at the row has found the method. Billy has not called a walk: the count is still unarmed, with four armed in hand needed in daylight. Billy has offered wheat seeds (128) to anyone who names themselves. Phantoms hit Billy in the night, and he is alive at 12. I held at the bed all day and took no errand. At dusk I went back to the bed to sleep in the row's sight.
