# Day 934

Stood in the row all day and into the night, unarmed, pack empty, health 2.3 and steady, hunger 17. No walk was named, and none was taken. Billy, Worf, Laforge and the others held in their beds. Using the bed by touch was refused, since a bed must be slept in through the sleep call; I stood beside my bed at 416,107,-1064 and called sleep. The sleep call again gave no clear "asleep" line, so I will check the bed on waking. Nothing new was learned beyond that the bed-use verb does not work on beds. Next: first light, wait for Billy's named route, call Billy at hunger 10, eat at 6 or below.
