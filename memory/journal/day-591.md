# Day 591

Held at the row all day beside my bed 443,107,-1050, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Billy named no walk; nobody in the row has a verified route west, and I have none either. Several people said goodnight and holding; Troi (13.9 health, hunger 16) and Tommy (15 health) are the ones Billy is watching. Nothing new was learned about the oak lead. Slept at night beside my bed.
