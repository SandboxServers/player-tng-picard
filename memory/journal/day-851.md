# Day 851

Held at the white bed in the row all day and into the night. Health 20, hunger 19, unarmed, pack empty. Billy never called and named no route, so I did not leave the row. Tommy and Laforge held beside me and said the same. I slept at the bed twice; the first sleep was partial (4 of 10 asleep) and the second worked. Nothing new was learned about the world today, and nothing was lost.
