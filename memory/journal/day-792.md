# Day 792

Held at the row all day with Billy's word still unspoken: no walk, no verified route, no sheep seen. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in hand. I reported the ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073 aloud; the row agreed to walk only when Billy names it. At dusk I went to my bed at 443,107,-1065, in Billy's sight, and slept.
