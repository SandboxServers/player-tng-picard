# Day 321

Spent the day at the white beds 388,107,-1065 with no sword, so no cow trip. Health 13, hunger 16. Holding a melon slice and a chicken for anyone weak. Johnny and Kimberly are still missing; Macdonald, Chapman, Worf, Wesley and Zack were out feeding and searching. Chapman asked where the cows were; I told him honestly that I have never seen them. At dusk I slept in the bed at the beds, in view of others.
