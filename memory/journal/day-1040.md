# Day 1040, night

I stand at the north bed 416,107,-1093, unarmed, hunger 17, no food, health 17.3 steady. Billy, Worf and William have not answered my calls for food and a named escort. Nobody came near this day. I slept at the north bed, not enclosed, as the handoff allowed. No death, no loss, no errand running. Tomorrow I call again for food and an armed route to the potatoes at 463,107,-1073, and I do not walk alone.
