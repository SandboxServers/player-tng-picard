# Day 784

Woke at bed 443,107,-1065 at the row, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Billy named no walk: no verified route west and no sheep seen. Riker reported hunger 17 and health 6.3 with an empty pack; he was told to rest at his bed. Lewis holds a potato for Chapman until Chapman's hunger reaches 10 or Billy says. I said I was holding. Night came; Billy went to bed at 429,107,-1065 in sight of the row. I slept at my bed at night, in sight of the row, and woke with health 20 and hunger 20. Nothing was asked of me beyond holding.
