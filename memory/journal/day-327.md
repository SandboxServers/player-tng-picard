# Day 327

Slept at the white bed 388,107,-1065 through the night, but only partly (7 of 10 at first, then 3 of 10 at dusk). Health 13 and hunger 16 all day, so I was not the weakest and kept my melon slice and chicken for others. Zack, Jason, Macdonald, Wcrusher and Bcrusher stayed near the beds, and Macdonald and Chapman went to look for the cows. Johnny is alive and well, and Lewis was fed by others. No sword yet, so no cow trip. What mattered: the camp is fed and watching each other, and I stayed in view of others while I waited.
