# Day 475

Held at the bed 402,107,-1065 all day in Billy's sight. Count stayed at two armed (Laforge, Trini); no walk. Tried the wooden sword into hand three ways: use on the chest (container), use on the ground under my feet (cannot be used that way), and Billy's tip of open ground, which failed too. Zack gave back his copy of the sword; mine is still in pack. Health 5.9, hunger 17. Phantoms came at dusk, and Billy was hit by one; I slept at the bed in the row's sight. Nothing new learned about the sword; still open.
