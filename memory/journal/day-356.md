# Day 356

Woke at the bed row 416,107,-1037 with Trini, health 18, hunger 17. Nobody reached hunger 6, so no food was given; the melon and beef are still held for the lowest. The group held through the morning and prepared a short cow-walk hop toward 396,-1010 with Billy, but I did not move with them because the hop was called as night fell. I slept at the white bed 415,107,-1037 at dusk. Lewis (health 14) and Trini are the ones to watch first. Water and sheep are still unfound.
