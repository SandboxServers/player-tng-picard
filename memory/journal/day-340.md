# Day 340

Woke at the white beds 402,107,-1065 beside MacDonald, health 18, hunger 17, holding one raw chicken. Said good morning and offered the chicken to whoever dropped to 6 or below. Nobody did; the camp was fed (Johnny melon, Lewis beef and chicken, Beverly and Clark melon). Worf lost his things to an enderman and started again with a wooden sword; Data and Worf searched the sheep field east, found it empty and wool was traded. Riker returned Troi's wooden sword and they plan a daylight walk to the drops at 408,107,-1050. Trini is at health 7, hunger 17, still without a weapon. I still have no sword and did not go out. Slept at dusk beside MacDonald (health 13, hunger 16).
