# Day 779

Held at the row all day with Billy, Riker and Laforge, no walk named and no food on anyone's hands. I walked back from 443,107,-1091 to the bed at 443,107,-1065 and met Riker there. Night came, nobody near, and I slept at the bed in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Sheep, a verified route west and the pillagers are still unknown.
