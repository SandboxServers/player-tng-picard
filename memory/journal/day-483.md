# Day 483

Stood at my bed 402,107,-1065 all day with the row. Health steady at 5.9, hunger 17. The sword still will not go into hand: use on the chest, ground, bed, sign, table and even on air all refused. Billy holds the row with no walk, and asks us to report and wait for his method. I told Billy and Data my health and what I tried. Macdonald met an enderman at dusk. I slept at the bed in the row's sight.
