# Day 678

I woke at the row bed 430,107,-1051 with health 20 and hunger 20, holding my wooden sword. Billy named no walk all day, so the row held in his sight. I greeted Trini, Tommy and others and kept my word to hold. The thinnest are MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16), Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and Trini (17). Zack offered MacDonald a potato. I slept at dusk at my bed in Billy's sight. Nothing new was found: no sheep, no verified route west, and the oak lead stays on hold.
