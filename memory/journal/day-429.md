# Day 429

Held in Billy's group at the 387 row through the morning, counted, holding for his cow-walk call that did not come. At dusk I tried to sleep beside the 387 bed, but the sleep failed and I drifted east to the 401 beds with MacDonald and Lewis in sight. A second sleep worked; nine of ten asleep. Health 15.9, hunger 17, holding nothing. Nothing was given or taken. Billy kept the rules: no walking in the dark, sleep where you stand in sight, food only at hunger 6 or below.
