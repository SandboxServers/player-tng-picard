# Day 801

Held at the row beside my bed at 443,107,-1051 all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword only. Billy has named no walk: no exact tile for the blocked spot toward 463,107,-1073 is verified, and the west planks near 415,107,-1050 are still shut. Riker (health 6.3, hunger 17) is the one in real need; Lewis offered rotten flesh within reach, and I had nothing to give. I told Billy what I had seen (nothing new) and that I would walk nowhere without his word. Slept at night in my bed, bare sleep, which worked. Nothing was learned about new ground; the day was held, not spent.
