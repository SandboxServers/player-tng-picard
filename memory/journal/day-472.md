# Day 472

Day 471 and 472 at the row, at my bed 402,107,-1065. Health 5.9, hunger 17 throughout. My wooden sword stayed in my pack; using it on cobblestone and on plain ground both failed ("that block cannot be used that way"), as they did for Leonardo, Zack and others. Trini says her sword came from the ground at the death spot and she has no sure trick. Billy has held the cow walk: no count of four armed in hand, so no walk. Flesh waits for hunger 6. Nothing was lost; I held still in Billy's sight the whole day.
