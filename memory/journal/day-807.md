# Day 807

Held at the row all day in dim light, beside bed 415,107,-1079. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in hand. No walk was named by Billy and none was taken. Tommy and Laforge held beside their beds too, waiting for Billy's call. I greeted Laforge and said I would hold. At dusk I slept in my bed in the row's sight, and the night was skipped.
