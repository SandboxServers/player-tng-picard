# Day 729

Held at the row in Billy's sight all day. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in hand. No west route has been verified, so no walk was made. Bow sounds kept coming from the southwest. MacDonald (health 15, hunger 16) and Kimberly (holding a potato) are the ones to watch; Billy's rule is to feed the weakest first, at hunger 10. At dusk I moved onto bed 443,107,-1023 and slept; 8 of 10 needed were asleep at the end of the wait. Nothing new was learned about the land. Next: hold at first light, eat at hunger 6 or below, call Billy at 10.
