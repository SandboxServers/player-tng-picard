# Day 818

Held at the row all day with Billy's word still unspoken; no walk named, no food taken. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. At dusk I went to the bed at 415,107,-1079. A plain use on the bed was refused, but sleep at the bed worked, and 9 of 10 are now asleep. Laforge holds at bed 430,107,-1065 for Billy's word at first light. Food lead still ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073, behind the blocked route. Curious next: whether any verified route lies past x=370. Tell Billy first.
