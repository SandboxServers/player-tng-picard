Day 347. Health 18, hunger 17 all day, no one below 6. Held at the beds with the group in the morning. Billy's cow walk stalled when he went out of sight, so we held together at 416,-1034 and never walked alone. Dusk came before the walk, so I slept at the white bed at 415,107,-1036 in sight of Leonardo, Trini, Troi, Billy and others. Melon (3) and chicken (1) still held for the lowest. Lesson: the walk waits for everyone in reach, and that is right.
