# Day 1145

Woke at the north bed 416,107,-1093, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health steady at 17.3. Called to Billy, Worf, Zack and Trini for food and a daylight escort; no answer came. William holds at his bed 416,107,-1079, also empty. Nothing was walked and nothing was eaten. Slept at the north bed at dusk, not enclosed, as the handoff said.
