# Day 835

Held at the row by the white bed at 416,107,-1079 all day. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Tommy holds in the row and reported his dusk count; no word from Billy, no walk named, Billy not in sight. Dawn and dusk counts given by me and by Tommy. Thunder and dim light through the day. Nothing dropped, nothing hit me. At night I said my count and slept in the bed; the sleep worked for 6 of the 10 needed. Nothing new learned about routes, sheep or pillagers. Tomorrow: hold, call Billy at hunger 10, eat at 6 or below.
