# Day 457

Held at the row by the 387 beds, unarmed in hand, sword in pack. Beverly and Trini held watch too. No spider came in reach, so Billy called no walk. Hunger stayed at 17, health steady at 5.9. At dusk I went to the 387,-1050 bed and slept in sight of the row. Still no sword in hand; the spider method is the next try when a spider is near. Clark's planks came earlier, so I have the sword, and I owe Clark thanks.
