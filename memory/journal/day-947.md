# Day 947

Quiet day in the row. No route was named, no one went out, and no food came in. Health held at 2.3 and hunger fell from 17 to about 17 by dusk. Tommy and I both held at the beds. I spoke my dusk count aloud and went to sleep in the white bed at 415,107,-1079. Nothing new was learned today beyond confirming the row is steady and that Tommy keeps the same watch. Next: call Billy at hunger 10, eat at 6 or below if food arrives, never leave the row alone or unarmed.
