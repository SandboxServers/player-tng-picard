# Handoff (day 1147, dusk)

At the north bed 416,107,-1093, not enclosed. Health 17.3 steady, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty, no food. Called aloud for food and a daylight escort at morning; no answer through the day. Billy, Worf, Zack, Trini still silent.

Next: at first light, call again for food and a named route with two armed escorts. Walk only with them in sight. Eat at hunger 6 or below. Sleep only at north-facing beds.
