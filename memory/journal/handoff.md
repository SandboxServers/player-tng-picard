# Handoff (day 839, dawn)

At the white bed 416,107,-1079 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No errand out. Tommy and Laforge hold in the row. Billy has not been seen and has not named a walk. I told Tommy I am waiting for Billy's word.

Next: hold at the row. Call Billy at hunger 10. Eat at 6 or below. Never leave the pillager line alone. Sleep at the bed at night, in the row's sight.
