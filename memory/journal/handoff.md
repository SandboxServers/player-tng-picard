# Handoff (day 983, dusk)

At the north bed row, 416,107,-1093, north end, out of the pillager line. Health 17.3 steady, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty, no food. Said for food to Billy and Worf at night and again at morning; no answer. Clark holds beside me.

No errand running. Next at waking: say again for food; eat at hunger 6 or below if food arrives; call Billy at hunger 10. No walk without a named route and armed escort.
