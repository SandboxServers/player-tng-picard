# Handoff (day 824, night)

Standing beside the white beds at 416,107,-1079 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Tommy is also at the row, unarmed. Billy not in sight; no walk named.
Use on the bed failed (a bed must be slept in, not used), so I am going to sleep with the sleep verb next to it.
Food lead: ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073. Cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113.
Next: hold at the row. Call Billy at hunger 10. Eat at 6 or below. Never leave the pillager line alone.
