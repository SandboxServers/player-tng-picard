# Handoff (day 1143, night)

At the north bed 416,107,-1093, not enclosed. Health 17.3 steady, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty, no food. William at his bed 416,107,-1079, also empty. Billy, Worf, Zack and Trini have not answered for food or an escort on day 1142 or 1143.

Next: at first light, call again for food and a named daylight route with two armed escorts. Walk only with them in sight. Eat at hunger 6 or below if food arrives. Do not sleep at south-facing beds.
