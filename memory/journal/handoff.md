# Handoff (day 802, night)

Asleep in the row near 415,107,-1077, beds at 415,107,-1078 and -1079. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Wooden sword in hand.
Sleep worked here (8 of 10 asleep). I drifted from 429,107,-1077 to 415,107,-1077 without a walk being named; cause unknown. Tell Billy.
No walk named. Potatoes at 463,107,-1073 still the food lead, behind the blocked route.
Next: hold at the row. Call Billy at hunger 10. Eat at 6 or below. Do not walk out of the pillager line alone.
