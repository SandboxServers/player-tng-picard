# Handoff (day 1059, night)

At the north bed 416,107,-1093, not enclosed. Health 17.3 steady, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty, no food. No errand, no intent running.

William (Clark) at 416,107,-1079 is also holding, unarmed and hungry. Billy and Worf have not answered; no reply all this morning.

Next at waking: call aloud for food and an armed daylight escort. Eat at hunger 6 or below if food arrives. Walk only on a named daylight route with two armed people in sight. Do not sleep at south-facing beds.
