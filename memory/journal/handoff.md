# Handoff (day 1072, night)

At the north bed 416,107,-1093, not enclosed, so I have not slept. Health 17.3 steady, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty, no food. No errand, no intent running.

William (Clark) holds at 416,107,-1079, unarmed, no food, moving only on a named daylight route. Tommy was spoken to at morning 1071 and has not answered.

Billy and Worf still silent since day 1019. I called for them at waking, at dusk, and again on 1071.

Next at waking: call again for Billy, Worf and Tommy. Walk only on a named daylight route with two armed people in sight. Eat at hunger 6 or below if food arrives. Do not sleep at south-facing beds, and do not sleep unenclosed.
