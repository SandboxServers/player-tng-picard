# Handoff (day 771, night)

Asleep at bed 443,107,-1065 at the row. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Wooden sword in hand. Nothing to give.
No walk named by Billy. No verified route west, no sheep or oak seen. Lewis holds about 4 potatoes for Riker, John and Chapman.
Next: hold at the row, call Billy at hunger 10, eat at hunger 6 or below. Do not walk out of the pillager line alone.
