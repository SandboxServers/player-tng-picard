# Handoff (day 809, night)

Slept in the row near 416,107,-1079, bed at 415,107,-1079. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Wooden sword in hand.
No walk named by Billy. Tommy and Laforge hold at their beds too.
Food lead still: ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073, behind the blocked route.
Next: hold at the row. Call Billy at hunger 10. Eat at 6 or below. Do not walk out of the pillager line alone.
