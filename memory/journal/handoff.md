# Handoff (day 824, night)

At the white bed 415,107,-1079 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Billy not in sight; no walk named. Tommy, Laforge and Riker hold at the row too.
Food lead: ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073. Cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113.
Next: hold at the row. Call Billy at hunger 10. Eat at 6 or below. Never leave the pillager line alone. Sleep in the bed in Billy's sight.
