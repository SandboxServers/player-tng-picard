# Handoff (day 841, going to bed)

At the white bed 416,107,-1079 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No errand out, no intent running. Tommy and Laforge also hold at the row; Laforge sleeping at bed 430,107,-1065.

Beverly: Tommy does not know her. Billy has not come into sight.

Next: hold at the row. Call Billy at hunger 10. Eat at 6 or below. Never leave the pillager line alone. Sleep at the bed at night, in the row's sight.
