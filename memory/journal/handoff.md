# Handoff (day 834, dawn)

At the white bed 415,107,-1079 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No errand out. Tommy and Laforge hold at the row; no walk named by Billy. Last words: my dawn count to the row.
Next: hold. Call Billy at hunger 10. Eat at 6 or below. Never leave the pillager line alone. Sleep at the bed at night.
