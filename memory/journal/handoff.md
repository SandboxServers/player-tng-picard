# Handoff (day 973-974)

At the northmost row bed, 416,107,-1093 (bed 415,107,-1093). Health 17.3, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty, no food. Not enclosed.
No walk named by Billy or Worf. No food offered. Clark holds beside me at the north end.
Pillagers are south of the beds. Stay north, out of their line. Never leave the row alone or unarmed. Do not sleep in an open bed.
Next: when Billy or Worf names a route with armed escort, follow it. Eat at hunger 6 or below if food arrives. Call Billy again at dawn.
