Day 659. Held at my bed 416,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, no food in pack. Billy named no walk, so nobody left the row. Told the row I have seen no sheep. Nobody has reached hunger 10 yet except MacDonald near 16 and Deanna near 16; Clark, Trini and Tommy hold potatoes for the hungriest. Slept at dusk in Billy's sight. The oak lead and the west route remain on hold.
