# Day 733

Held in Billy's row all day at bed 443,107,-1023 with health 20 and hunger 20, no food. Nobody named a walk. Bow sounds came again from the southwest, far off. Kimberly stood beside me with a potato. At night Billy, MacDonald and Troi said goodnight, and I slept at my bed in their sight; the night was skipped. Nothing new was found, and the route past x=370 is still unverified.
