# Day 1053

Still at the north bed 416,107,-1093, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17 and health steady at 17.3. I called aloud at dawn for food and an armed escort toward the potatoes at 463,107,-1073. Only William answered, and he is also unarmed with no food. Billy and Worf have been silent since day 1019. I did not walk alone, which was right. Night came with thunder and I slept at the north bed. Nothing new arrived.
