# Day 1027

Slept at the north bed, 416,107,-1093, not enclosed. Health 17.3 steady, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty, no food. Billy and Worf have not answered my calls; William (Clark) answered from his bed at 416,107,-1079 and holds too, unarmed, waiting for Billy or Worf to name a route. No walk, no errand. The row is holding, but hunger is the real danger: the food leads (potatoes at 463,107,-1073, cows near 434,106,-1113) need a named route and armed escort that has not come.
