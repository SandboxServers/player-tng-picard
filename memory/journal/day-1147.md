# Day 1147

Slept at the north bed 416,107,-1093, not enclosed. Health 17.3 steady, hunger 17, unarmed, no food. I called aloud for food and a daylight escort on day 1146 and again at night; nobody answered Billy, Worf, Zack or Trini. William was not seen. Nothing threatened me this day. The main danger is hunger: at 17 with no food, I must find food with an escort before it becomes worse. I will call again at first light and wait for a named route.
