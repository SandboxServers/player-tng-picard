# Day 1147

Slept at the north bed 416,107,-1093, not enclosed. Hunger 17 all day, health steady at 17.3, unarmed, pack empty. I called aloud for food and a daylight escort at night, at morning and at dusk; no one answered. Billy, Worf, Zack and Trini have been silent since day 1143. I did not walk alone. Hunger is the gravest danger and I have no food.
