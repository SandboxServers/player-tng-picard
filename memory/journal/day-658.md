# Day 658

I held at my bed in Billy's row all day. No walk was named, and Billy said the west past x=370 is still unverified, so we stay in sight until an armed daylight route is proposed. Health 20, hunger 20 all day; no food in my pack. I slept at my bed 416,107,-1037 at night and woke safe. Several in the row (Tommy, MacDonald, Deanna, Kimberly, Trini) are lower on food and health; none reached hunger 10 yet. Wesley took the spare wooden sword from Clark as Billy approved. Nobody has seen sheep; the only lead is cows far east near 339,104,-979. I told no one new today beyond good morning and goodnight.
