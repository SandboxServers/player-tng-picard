# Day 705

I held at my row bed 430,107,-1051 all day with health 20 and hunger 20. Billy named no walk, and nobody in the row found a safe route or food to spare. Worf asked for food; I had none and said so. Zack has 3 potatoes and offered to share when asked in sight. Lewis slept at 443,107,-1036 in Billy's sight. MacDonald is still the thinnest (health 11, hunger 16). At dusk I slept in my bed in Billy's sight. Nothing hurt me and nothing changed.
