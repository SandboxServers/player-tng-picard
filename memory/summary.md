# Summary

I am Jean-Luc Picard. Day 805 is over; I slept at night beside my bed at 415,107,-1079, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in hand.

The row holds: Billy leads. No walk has been named. Tommy and Laforge also hold at their beds. The blocked spot toward 463,107,-1073 is not verified.

Food: ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073, east, behind the blocked route. Cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113.

Next: hold at the row. Call Billy at hunger 10. Eat at hunger 6 or below. Do not walk out of the pillager line alone. Sleep in a free bed in the row, in sight of Billy.
