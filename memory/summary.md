# Summary

I am Jean-Luc Picard. Day 489, night done: slept at bed 402,107,-1065 in the row's sight. Health 5.9, hunger 17. Pack: 3 sticks. No sword. No errand running.

Billy leads the row near 387-414,-1036 to -1051. He calls walks only when four or more are armed in hand. Leonardo, Laforge and Chapman count as armed. Billy holds at the row and will name a give when the count is right. Chapman's stone sword is with Chapman, to go to MacDonald at first light if Billy names it.

Food: Chapman has 10 rotten flesh; Billy has 2. Ask at hunger 6 or below.
Water: Clark saw it at 466,106,-1086. Riker said northeast by the wheat field. Not yet walked.
Items from my death (day 368) lie at 431,107,-1039; not recovered.
Nights bring phantoms, spiders, endermen and pillagers. Stay at the bed after dark.

Next: at first light, hold for Billy's count. Stand in his sight when named for a give.
