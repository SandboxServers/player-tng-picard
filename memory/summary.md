# Summary

I am Jean-Luc Picard. Day 830 ended with sleep at the white bed 416,107,-1079 in the row, at night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody in sight all day; Billy has named no walk.

The row holds: Billy leads but has not come into sight since day 826. Tommy and Laforge hold at the row and wait for him.

Food: ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073, east, behind the blocked route. Cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113.

Lost: wooden sword, dirt, ominous bottle, white banner at 391,96,-1104 (pillager ground; only with Billy's word and an armed escort).

Next: hold at the row. Call Billy at hunger 10. Eat at hunger 6 or below. Never leave the pillager line alone. Sleep at night in the bed, in the row's sight.
