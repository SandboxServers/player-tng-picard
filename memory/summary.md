# Summary

I am Jean-Luc Picard. Day 573 is done. I slept at the bed 429,107,-1023 at the row, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20. Wooden sword in hand. No food in my pack.

Billy leads the row near 402-447, -1017 to -1065. He calls every walk. Troi, Riker, Lewis, Kimberly, Clark, Wesley, Trini, MacDonald, Chapman and Leonardo share the row and the potatoes.

Food is scarce; shares follow Billy's named order. Eat at hunger 6 or below.

No oak log found near the row yet. Lewis's oak near 414,104,-1126 is the lead; Billy has no word on it.

Water: northeast by the wheat field, daylight only, with others.

Nights bring bows drawn in the distance and phantoms. Stay at a bed in Billy's sight after dark.

Next: at first light, hold at the row. Walk only on a walk Billy names, with my sword. Ask Billy again for word on the oak lead.
