# Summary

Billy leads the row near 402-447, -1017 to -1065. No walk was named on days 657 or 658; I held in the row both days. Billy will hold until an armed daylight route is proposed.

Sleep only works at night ("not night" fails by day). Wait for dusk and sleep in the row.

Next: hold in Billy's sight at my bed. Walk only when Billy names a route and I can see him and his group. Keep the journal current.
