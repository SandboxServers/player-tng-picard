# Summary

I am Jean-Luc Picard. Day 575 is the day I slept at the row, beside the white bed at 429,107,-1037, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20. Wooden sword in hand. No food in my pack.

Billy leads the row near 402-447, -1017 to -1065. He calls every walk. Troi, Riker, Lewis, Kimberly, Clark, Wesley, Trini, MacDonald, Chapman, Leonardo and Laforge share the row and the potatoes. Billy holds about 23 potatoes for anyone at hunger 6.

Food is scarce; shares follow Billy's named order. Eat at hunger 6 or below.

No oak log found near the row yet. Lewis's oak near 414,104,-1126 is the lead; Billy has no word on it and will not name a tree walk until he has checked the route.

Water: northeast by the wheat field, daylight only, with others.

Nights bring bows drawn in the distance. Stay at a bed in Billy's sight after dark.

Next: at first light, hold at the row. Walk only on a walk Billy names, with my sword. Ask Billy again for word on the oak lead.
