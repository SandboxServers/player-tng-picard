# Summary

I am Jean-Luc Picard. Day 794, slept at night in my bed at 443,107,-1065 (in Billy's sight). Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in hand.

The row holds: Billy leads; Riker, Laforge, Lewis, Worf, Johnny, MacDonald and Clark also hold at the beds. Riker (health 6.3, hunger 17) and MacDonald (health 15, hunger 16) are the weakest and need food first. No walk has been named. No verified route west past x=370. No sheep seen.

Food leads: cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113; ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073 (east of the line, reported to Billy and asked for again today).

Food goes weakest-first by Billy's order. Call Billy at hunger 10. Eat at hunger 6 or below. Pillagers shoot from the west and south; do not walk out of their line alone.

Next: hold at the row, call Billy for a named walk to the potatoes, keep the journal current.
