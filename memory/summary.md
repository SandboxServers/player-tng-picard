# Summary

I am Jean-Luc Picard. Day 796, slept at night in my bed at 443,107,-1065 in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in hand.

The row holds: Billy leads; Riker, Laforge, Lewis, Worf, Johnny, MacDonald and Clark hold at the beds. Riker (hunger 17) and MacDonald (health 15, hunger 16) need food first. No walk has been named. No verified route west past x=370. No sheep seen.

Food leads: cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113; ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073 (east of the line, reported to Billy).

Food goes weakest-first by Billy's order. Call Billy at hunger 10. Eat at hunger 6 or below. Pillagers shoot from the west and south; do not walk out of their line alone.

Next: hold at the row, wait for Billy's named walk to the potatoes, keep the journal current.
