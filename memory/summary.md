# Summary

I am Jean-Luc Picard. Day 815 ended at dusk, and I did not sleep: the bed at 415,107,-1079 gave no sleep record. I stand in the row at about 416,107,-1079, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.

The row holds: Billy leads. No walk has been named. Tommy and Laforge hold too, each with an empty pack.

Food: ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073, east, behind the blocked route. Cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113.

Lost: wooden sword, dirt, ominous bottle, white banner at 391,96,-1104 (pillager ground; only with Billy's word and an armed escort).

Next: hold at the row. Call Billy at hunger 10. Eat at hunger 6 or below. Never leave the pillager line alone. Try the bed again at the next dusk, using sleep at the bed.
