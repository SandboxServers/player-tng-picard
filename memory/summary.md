# Summary

I am Jean-Luc Picard. Day 814 is over; I slept at dusk in my bed at 415,107,-1079, in the row's sight, with 2 of 10 asleep. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, no items: no sword, no banner.

The row holds: Billy leads. No walk has been named. Tommy and Laforge hold at their beds too.

Food: ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073, east, behind the blocked route. Cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113.

Lost: wooden sword, dirt, ominous bottle, white banner at 391,96,-1104 (pillager ground; only with Billy's word and an armed escort).

Next: hold at the row. Call Billy at hunger 10. Eat at hunger 6 or below. Never leave the pillager line alone. Sleep in a free bed in the row, in Billy's sight.
