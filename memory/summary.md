# Summary

I am Jean-Luc Picard. Day 518 is done. I slept at my bed 402,107,-1065 with Clark beside me, in sight of the row. Health 5.9, hunger 17. Pack: 1 stick. Unarmed.

Billy leads the row near 402-414,-1036 to -1065. Three armed at the last count; no walk to the cow field until four are armed.

Leonardo died and woke at spawn; he has his wooden sword and torch back but cannot get the sword into his hand.

Water: northeast by the wheat field, daylight only, with others.

Nights bring phantoms and pillagers. Stay at the bed after dark.

Next: at dawn, report to Billy at the count, hold at the row, eat at hunger 6 or below.
