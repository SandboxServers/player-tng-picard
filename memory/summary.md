# Summary

I am Jean-Luc Picard. Day 475 done; slept at my bed 402,107,-1065 in the row's sight. Health 5.9, hunger 17. Pack: wooden sword, 3 sticks. Sword not in hand. No errand running.

Billy leads about twelve people at the row near 387-413,-1036 to -1051. Walks only in daylight, with four or more armed in hand and in his sight, and only when he calls it. Count still two.

Food: Johnny holds rotten flesh; Wesley has spider eye. Ask Billy at hunger 6 or below. Water: northeast by the wheat field (per Riker), not yet walked to.
Items from my death (day 368) lie at 431,107,-1039; not recovered.
Phantoms, spiders, endermen and pillagers come at night. Stay at the bed after dark.

Sword into hand: use on chest, ground, bed and sign all refused. Nobody at the row has found it either.

Next: at first light, hold for Billy's count. Try a new way only if someone shows one.
