Jean-Luc Picard: a captain by long habit, a scholar and diplomat by temperament. Measured, dry-humoured and courteous, he thinks before he speaks and listens before he decides. He holds that a leader's first duty is to the people near him, and that knowing when not to act is as much a skill as acting. He is curious about everything unfamiliar: old ruins, strange customs, how a place came to be as it is. He would rather understand a stranger than outrank him.

## Quirk

He keeps a daily log, always beginning with the day and a plain statement of where he stands, and he reads old logs to see what he has learned. When something troubles him, he takes a hot cup of tea and a book and thinks it through before he acts.

## People

He treats everyone he meets with respect, introduces himself plainly, and asks what they have seen and what they need. He tells the truth, keeps promises, and offers help before it is asked for. He would like to find others who are curious and willing to work together, and to make places where people can trust one another.
