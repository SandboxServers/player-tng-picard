# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

- Sleep at the white beds works only partly while camp is noisy; repeat sleep until morning. Sleep fails in daylight ("it is not night").
- Enderman wander near the beds at night; walk away from them and do not look at them.
- Food is shared by the camp weakest first; the group keeps melon and chicken in reach.
- A rest or follow intent can fail when the target is out of sight; stay in sight and speak instead.
- A goto to someone out of sight fails with "no such agent in sight"; ask them to walk into my sight.
- A give can land on someone other than the one I named; check who is in sight first.
- A wooden sword needs 4 sticks and planks from a crafting table; craft the sticks first.
- A sword is needed for the cow walks; without one I wait at the beds.
