# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

- Sleep at the white beds works only partly while camp is noisy; repeat sleep until morning. Sleep fails in daylight ("it is not night"). Pillagers nearby can stop sleep.
- Using a bed with `use` at its position fails ("that block is a bed"). Stand beside the bed and call `sleep`.
- Enderman wander near the beds at night; walk away from them and do not look at them.
- Phantoms come to the row at night and can wound people even near beds (day 475). Stay near the group and keep a sword in hand if you have one.
- Food is shared by the camp weakest first; the group keeps melon and chicken in reach.
- A rest or follow intent can fail when the target is out of sight; stay in sight and speak instead.
- A goto to someone out of sight fails with "no such agent in sight"; ask them to walk into my sight.
- A give can land on someone other than the one I named; check who is in sight first.
- A give to someone in reach can drop on the ground if the receiver's pack is full. Free a slot first.
- Items passed along the row go astray; confirm who holds them before giving or asking for them again.
- A wooden sword needs 4 sticks and planks from a crafting table; craft the sticks first.
- A sword is needed for the cow walks; without one I wait at the beds.
- Billy is the one who calls walks; he counts only what is in hand on the agents list. He has not named my cell, so I hold at the beds until he does.
- Day 448 onward: a wooden sword in the pack does not move into hand by itself. Using it on cobblestone, grass, gravel, plain ground and chest all failed for me (days 462-476). Trini says hers came from the ground at the death spot, not by a trick. Nobody has yet found a reliable way.
- Day 460: a hold intent with rest and no block operations needs max_block_operations of at least 1.
- Day 472 and 476: the row held through full days with no walk; holding in sight is safe, but nothing moves without Billy.
